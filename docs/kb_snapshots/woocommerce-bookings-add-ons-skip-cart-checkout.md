# Add-ons for Bookings And Appointments for WooCommerce – Skip Cart & Checkout

In this article, you can learn how to allow customers to book the product for a particular date/time without having to go through the cart and checkout. For example: Allow users to book the product for free. We will cover the [Bookings and Appointments for WooCommerce](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/) plugin, along with **Skip Checkout for Bookings** **Add-on** and how these two work together to help you book a date/time range by skipping the cart and checkout.

* * *

## Skip Checkout for Bookings add-on

* * *

### What does it do?

This add-on allows the customer to place a successful booking without having to traverse through the cart and checkout process. The setup is quite simple, as shown below.

* * *

#### Pre-Requisites

In order to use the addon, you are required to have the following plugins installed on your WooCommerce store.

  * [Bookings and Appointments for WooCommerce plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/)**[Version 1.6.5 and above]**
  * [Skip Checkout for Bookings add-on](https://www.pluginhive.com/tailor-made-woocommerce-bookings-solutions/#skip_checkout)

* * *

#### Allow users to book the product without redirecting to the cart and checkout pages

 _**The organization wants to provide free meals to people on a daily basis. They do not want the customers to pay for the meal slot. Hence, they want to skip the process of navigating to the cart and then checkout to place successful bookings.**_

To set up this requirement, follow the steps below :

  * Download the Skip Checkout for Bookings add-on and install it on your WooCommerce store
  * Visit the desired bookable product on the shop page
  * Select a booking slot for the product and click on the **Book Now** button, as shown in the screenshot below
