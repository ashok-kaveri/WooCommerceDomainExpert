# Add WooCommerce Canada Post Insurance cost to Shipping Rates

In the modern world of E-commerce, shipping comes with a lot of risks. There are chances that while shipping, your product may be damaged or lost. In such scenarios, it becomes mandatory for online store owners to have some way of ensuring the product cost. WooCommerce store owners have similar requirements when it comes to shipping. According to Joseph, who owns a WooCommerce-based automotive parts store, “ _**…Is there any way I can ensure my products while shipping..? I ship with Canada Post and it is extremely important for me to have the cost of insurance included in the estimate from Canada Post. Is there any way to make this work using your plugin..?**_ “

In this article, we will discuss how using the [**WooCommerce Canada Post Shipping plugin**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-canada-post-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/), Joseph can add the insurance amount to his shipping rates.

## Setting up Insurance in the plugin settings

The WooCommerce Canada Post Shipping plugin has a built-in option to add coverage. While on the plugin settings page, you will find the option under the **Additional Options** field. There you can find the option for **Coverage.** The image below shows the option to add coverage in the WooCommerce Canada Post Shipping plugin.

Once you enable this option, the package will be subjected to coverage under Canada Post. After adding the product to the cart, you can even check whether the product is eligible for coverage by checking the Canada Post Debug Response, by enabling the debug mode.
