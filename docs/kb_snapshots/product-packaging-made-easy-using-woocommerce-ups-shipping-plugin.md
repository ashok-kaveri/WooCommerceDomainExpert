# Simplify Product Packaging with WooCommerce UPS Plugin

[**WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin with Print Label**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/) comes packed with a ton of amazing features, and one of them is the parcel packing method. In this article, we will tell you how to use this feature and how it can help you with your daily business.

If you are running an online store, you might be familiar with the troubles of shipping. One of them is not being able to pack your products properly. With so many products, it is almost impossible to properly pack your products best possible way, so that it won’t cost you a fortune for shipping. Different products will have different dimensions and weights. Hence, it becomes very important to know how you can pack them without wasting unnecessary space. This article will cover one of the most amazing features of the **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)**. Using the plugin, WooCommerce store owners can get an idea of how they can pack their products.

WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin displays real-time shipping rates directly from **[UP](https://www.pluginhive.com/ups-shipping/)** S. These rates are based on factors like package weight and dimensions, the number of packages, and the distance between the source and destination. Most importantly, the WooCommerce UPS plugin displays the content of each package to help store owners pack their products together. This way it becomes very easy to pack the products in the best possible way. Now, before getting into the details of how the plugin does that, first, let’s get an idea about the plugin itself.

As a WooCommerce store owner, once a customer places an order, you have to verify the order and create a package. The package will contain all the products purchased by the customer. ### Package Details

As discussed, in the case of multiple packages containing multiple products, store owners can easily get confused about packing products into packages. There may be 2 different products with the same weight yet different dimensions. Or even all the dimensions can be the same except one. In such cases, it is very important to have an idea of which products are packed in which package and with which other products.

**[WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** shows the details of all the packages that are generated by the plugin. It contains details like,

  * **Name of the products in that package**
  * **Total Weight of the package**
  * **The quantity of the products in that package**

### Benefits of Getting Package Details

**[WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** provides you with an easy way to show which products will you pack together. This has a lot of benefits when it comes to shipping products to your customers. Some of the benefits of displaying package details using the WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin are:

  * **Easy Packing**

One of the most common things that confuse store owners is how to pack the products when there are multiple products in an order. **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** provides an easy way to display how you can pack your products. One can clearly say that the store owners can easily pack their products and ship it to the customers. Especially, when they know which products would be packed together in one box and which ones in another box.

  * **Saves Additional Cost on Shipping**

While shipping rates are calculated based on factors like the number of packages, package weight and dimensions, and the destination address, packing your products can also help you decrease your shipping cost. For Example, the customers pay the shipping rates that are returned by the plugin. However, the **[WooCommerce UPS plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** calculates rates based on the packages that it has generated based on the packing method selected in the plugin settings. In such a case, if while packing your products, the package weight does not match the one calculated by the plugin, it may increase the overall shipping cost.

  * **Faster Packing**

Using the **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** , the store owners do not need to figure out which product to pack in which package. It also shows if multiple products need to be packed together in the same package or in another package. It reduces the total time required to pack the products in their respective boxes, hence, making the process of packing products much faster.
