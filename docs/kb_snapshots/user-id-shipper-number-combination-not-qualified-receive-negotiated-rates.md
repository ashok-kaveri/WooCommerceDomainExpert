# Use UPS Negotiated Rates with WooCommerce Shipping

Getting accurate shipping rates is essential for a smooth checkout experience and customer satisfaction. Sometimes, users may encounter issues where negotiated UPS rates are not displayed as expected. One such message refers to a mismatch between the User ID and Shipper Number.

In this small guide, we will tell you how you can make use of the UPS Negotiated Rates when using the **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin with Print Label](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)**. Read below to know more.

* * *

## **Customer:**

We’re trying to enable shipping on an online shopping cart using the UPS plugin and we keep getting this error: “User ID and Shipper Number combination is not qualified to receive negotiated rates.” I have everything enabled as per your FAQ/Troubleshooting, including negotiated rates, and it continues to display the error.

* * *

## **PluginHive Support:**

UPS Account-Specific Negotiated Rates are special rates you get on your account. In order to get these rates on your account, you need to make arrangements with your UPS representative. Enable the Negotiated Rates checkbox in the plugin settings, which will let your store display the negotiated rates to your customers.

* * *

* * *

Before this, make sure that you have enabled negotiated rates for your UPS account. Read more about [UPS Account-Specific Negotiated Rates](https://www.ups.com/in/en/help-center/technology-support/worldship/negotiated-rates.page).

**Note:**   
The User ID should match the UPS account where the negotiated rates are enabled. You can also read this guide and learn [ **How to Show/Hide UPS Negotiated Rates** ](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/show-hide-ups-negotiated-rates/).
