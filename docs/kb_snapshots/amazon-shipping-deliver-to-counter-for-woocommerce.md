# Amazon Shipping Deliver to Counter for WooCommerce

This document outlines how to integrate Amazon Shipping Deliver to Counter service with your WooCommerce store using the [WooCommerce Shipping Services Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/woocommerce-shipping-services/). This feature allows customers to select a pickup point at a nearby counter (e.g., post office or courier facility) for added convenience and security.

* * *

## Overview of Deliver to Counter

Deliver to Counter enables packages to be delivered to a secure counter rather than directly to the customer’s address. It provides flexibility for customers who are unavailable for doorstep delivery and ensures better security by reducing the risk of theft. It’s also useful when there are address issues.

* * *

## Advantages

  * **Flexible Pickup** : Customers can choose a convenient pickup location.
  * **Increased Security** : Reduced theft risk by storing packages at a secure counter.
  * **Cost-Effective** : Often cheaper than doorstep delivery, especially in remote areas.
  * **Reduced Delivery Failures** : Solves issues with incorrect addresses or unavailable recipients.

* * *

## Steps to Enable Amazon Shipping Deliver to Counter on your WooCommerce Store

** _Note_**  
  

**Deliver to Counter (D2C) must be activated in your Amazon shipping account before using this feature.** Please get in touch with your Amazon shipping account representative or customer support to enable D2C on your account.

_*This feature is only available in United Kingdom._

### 1\. Install WooCommerce Shipping Services Plugin

Install and activate the “[WooCommerce Shipping Services](https://www.pluginhive.com/woocommerce-shipping-services/)” plugin from the WordPress admin dashboard. This plugin is necessary for integrating Amazon Shipping with your WooCommerce store.

_**Reference:** [How to install the WooCommerce Shipping Services Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/setting-up-woocommerce-shipping-services/#download_install_activate_plugin)_

* * *

### 2\. Configure Amazon Shipping Account

To seamlessly link your **Amazon Shipping account** with your WooCommerce store, simply configure your account in the plugin settings. For step-by-step instructions on integrating Amazon Shipping, please refer to our comprehensive guide: [Amazon Shipping Guide for WooCommerce](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/amazon-shipping-guide-for-woocommerce/).

* * *

### 3\. Enable Deliver to Counter Option

  * Navigate to **WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping** **> PluginHive WooCommerce Shipping Services**.

  * To allow your customers to have their orders **delivered to a counter** , please enable the “**Local Pickup Location** ” setting by checking the box.

  * **Save Settings**.

* * *

## 4\. Deliver to Counter Service on WooCommerce Checkout

During checkout, customers will input their shipping address and can select a nearby pickup location. They proceed with the order after selecting the pickup option.

* * *

## 5\. Generate and Print Shipping Labels

On the left-side menu, navigate to the ‘**Shipping** ‘ option to open the plugin. Here, all the orders in your WooCommerce store are displayed.

Select the order for which you want to generate shipping labels and click on ‘**Generate Labels** ‘.

After generating, click “**Print Label** ” to download the shipping label in PDF format.
