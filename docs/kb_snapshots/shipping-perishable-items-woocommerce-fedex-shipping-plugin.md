This guide will take you through the steps required to ship perishable items via FedEx. WooCommerce is one of the best eCommerce platforms that allow business owners to sell items. You might already know that shipping products like a t-shirt and electronic items are quite an easy job. However, the story changes when the store owner has to ship perishable items to their customers. These types of items are very delicate and need special attention. Store owners have to take into account all the possible things that can go wrong with mailing these items. To ship perishable items, you need to follow proper rules and guidelines prescribed by the shipping carriers and the local laws. You should also follow the suggested packing methods that will ensure the safe arrival of these items.

As you might be thinking, the process of delivering the perishable items would be quite long and risky, then that is not the case. If you are shipping your items using the FedEx services then this article will help you a lot.

* * *

Following are the most important steps that you would need to follow.

#### 1\. Check the Shipping rules and regulations of the destination state or country

There are many country-based shipping laws that may interfere with your shipment. And with the increase in the number of cross-border orders, store owners should be more aware of the shipping regulations. To find out whether you are allowed to ship certain perishable items to a specific country, you should quickly search for them on the internet. Some of the questions may be like are you allowed to send gourmet ice cream to Iceland? Or are you allowed to mail artisanal sausage to Thailand?

You should not make any assumptions in these cases as they might lead you to a bigger problem. For example, Australia does not allow any import of specific meat and non-meat products like seeds, fruits, vegetables, nuts, dairy, and some other products as well. You are also not allowed to ship these products mixed with other items. Like bakery items that may contain a certain amount of milk and egg products. However, if you can obtain special permission from the officials then you are eligible to ship these items.

* * *

#### 2\. Choose the right packaging for perishable items

If you deliver melted butter, then there is a high chance that your receiver might not come back for another order. This is one of the reasons why your package should be properly insulated from extreme cold or heat. For instance, the United States Department of Agriculture suggests that all perishable items that arrive in the package should be at or below 4.5 degrees Celcius. In order to ship temperature-sensitive items, you need to make sure that the package has a coolant.

One of the most common types of refrigerant used is dry ice. But, they are considered hazardous for air travel. Thus, the shipments containing dry ice must be properly labeled. The shipment should also comply with the shipping carrier’s rules and regulations. To provide proper insulation, you should choose the right insulators for your packages. Some of the most common insulations used are usually made from,

  * **Styrofoam Boxes**
  * **Styrofoam Cut Sheets**
  * **Insulated Liners**
  * **Air filled Insulation Liners**
  * **Insulated Pads**

#### 3\. Add the appropriate shipping label on the package

Both your customers and the shipping partner should be aware of the fact that you are shipping food items with dry ice. This way they will not deliver the package without any precautions like keeping the package out of the refrigerated room for long hours. Moreover, you should clearly mention that the package contains dry ice. This is because dry ice is considered as dangerous and hazardous by almost all shipping carriers. The following image shows the sample label that clearly says that the parcel contains dry ice.

Another important thing to note is that the label should contain the **ASR**(Adult Signature Required) term. This signifies that the package requires an adult signature. You can [refer the following video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GzgQqh0CX1A) to see how it is done.

* * *

#### 4\. Select the optimum shipping service

You may think that you should choose the service that is the fastest among the others. However, you should also know that these services are usually the most expensive ones. For example, the FedEx 2 Day will cost less than that the FedEx Standard Overnight. So, if you are selling food items that can easily survive the 2-day trip then you should go for the more economical ones. The FedEx Shipping Plugin allows you to show certain shipping services on the Cart/Checkout page. This way you can allow customers to choose the selected services that you would want your parcel to ship with.
