#### **Displaying Live and Accurate Canada Post Shipping rates on the WooCommerce checkout page**

* * *

* * *

Well, the most important feature, that store owners want from a shipping plugin is that it can get a shipping rate quote from Canada Post. The **[WooCommerce Canada Post Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-canada-post-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/) by PluginHive** is based on the**official Canada Post API**.

In simple words, the plugin sends a request to the official Canada Post server, based on the package details such as the weight of the package, source, destination for shipment, etc. Then the plugin will receive the shipping cost from the Canada Post servers, which will be displayed on the cart page. The plugin also provides options to enable **Contract Shipping Rates** , if you are eligible for that.

One thing that **Canada Post Shipping Method by WooCommerce** can do pretty well is to calculate shipping rates. Similar to the **[WooCommerce Canada Post Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-canada-post-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** , this plugin too gets the rates from official Canada Post servers and then displays them on the cart page.

#### **Ability to Choose the Most Efficient Packaging Method**

Apart from shipping, product packing is one of the most important tasks. It requires some advanced algorithms to check the weight and dimensions of different products and to suggest packing for the same.

**The[WooCommerce Canada Post Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-canada-post-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/) by PluginHive** is one of the best options when it comes to product packing. It uses three parcel packing methods that give storeowners different choices to pack their products based on their preferences. These methods include,

  * **Pack Items Individually  
** This method packs the products individually. In other words, the number of packages is equal to the number of items in the cart.
  * **Box Packing with Custom Weights and Dimensions**  
This method allows store owners to create packing boxes with custom weight and dimensional limits. Based on the weight and dimensions, the products are packed in a particular box.
  * **Weight-Based packing  
** This is the best packing method that can accommodate almost any shipping scenario based on weight. Using this method store owners can pack any number of products up to a weight limit, inside a single box.

Similarly, the **Canada Post Shipping Method by WooCommerce** packs the products on the basis of the packing methods shown above. These methods are very helpful when it comes to providing stores with the best options for packing.
