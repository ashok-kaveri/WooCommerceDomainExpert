## WooCommerce Shipping

WooCommerce offers a lot of easy options to prepare and deliver orders to your customers. Let us take a quick look at these delivery options :

  * **Flat Rate:** Under the Flat Rate option, the store owner has to fix a standard shipping rate for the products. The weight or dimension of the order has no effect on the charges fixed by the store owner. The shipping cost will remain the same and the customer can place as many orders as he wants.
  * **Free Shipping** : Under this option, the store has the responsibility of delivering the order to the customers free of cost. This means the store cannot claim any charges from the customers.
  * **International Delivery** : Just like a Flat Rate, in terms of International Delivery the store owner fixes a standard rate to deliver the products to an international location. For example, ordering a product from the UK to the USA may result in extra charges which come under International Delivery.
  * **Local Delivery** : As the name suggests, under Local Delivery the source, as well as the destinations of the product, are within the same geographical area.
  * **Local Pickup** : Things are a little different when it comes to Local Pickup. With Local Pickup, the customers, and not the store owner comes to pick up the order. As a result, this option is least preferred by the customers.

Apart from these WooCommerce Shipping options, there is a variety of other options too. But those options are based on the shipping services(UPS, FedEx, USPS, etc.,), and varies from one service to another.

Once you figure out the suitable option for you and your customer and set up the rates accordingly, then comes the product packaging and finding a suitable service that will help you deliver the product.

## WooCommerce Shipping Labels

**WooCommerce Shipping labels** contain all the key information necessary for every element of the supply chain to move the package from one hand to another and from the warehouse to your customer’s doorstep. It also contains tracking information that both store owners, as well as customers, can use to track their packages.

When it comes to shipping services, there are many carriers that deliver shipments from your warehouse to your customer’s doorstep.

Some of the leading companies in this field are [UPS](https://www.pluginhive.com/ups-shipping/), [FedEx](https://www.pluginhive.com/fedex-shipping/), [DHL](https://www.pluginhive.com/dhl-express/), and [USPS](https://www.pluginhive.com/usps/). One thing which is common with every one of them is that getting a shipping label is a tiring and tedious task if you do it manually.

If a store owner wants to deliver a product to the customer, then he or she has to visit the official website of the company that is offering him/her shipping services. There, he/she will have to log in every single time an order is placed and he will have to enter the order details to get a shipping label.
