# WooCommerce Bookings Add-ons – Conditional Discount based on Participants

In this article, we will check out how you can provide a **[booking discount](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/discount-woocommerce-bookings-an-ultimate-guide/)** for your guests based on your preferences. We will cover amusement park booking with children and adults as booking guests/participants. This article will help you set up a booking discount for a certain number of adults based on how many children are visiting the park, with the help of the Participant Based Discount add-on plugin for the**[Bookings And Appointments for WooCommerce](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/) **plugin**.**

* * *

## Participant-based Discount Add-on plugin

#### What does it do?

This plugin allows you to provide a discount on a certain type of participant based on the number of other participants selected by the customer on the cart page.

#### Pre-Requisites:

  * ****[Bookings And Appointments for WooCommerce](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/)** [Version 1.1.15 and above]**
  * **Participants based Discount Add-on plugin**

**Business Case: Provide a discount on the number of adults based on the number of children**

 _I provide amusement park booking option to both the adults along with the children. However, I require my customers to have a discounted price in such a way that,_

  * _for 2 children, allow 1 adult to book free of cost_
  *  _for 4 children, allow 2 adults to book free of cost_
  *  _for 6 children, allow 3 adults to book free of cost_

**Solution:**

Follow the steps below to set up discount based on the participants

  * Install the plugin on your WooCommerce store
  * Visit the bookable product and click on **Edit**
  * Visit the **Bookings Participants** section and click on **Enable Participants
