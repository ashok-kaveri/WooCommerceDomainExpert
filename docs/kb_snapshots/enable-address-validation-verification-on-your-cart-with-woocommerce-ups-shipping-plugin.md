## WooCommerce Address Validation using PluginHive UPS Shipping plugin

The **WooCommerce Address Validation** feature provides direct automated integration between your WooCommerce website and UPS API. The API checks a customer’s address and provides suggested alternatives if an error is discovered.

Whether built into your public website or integrated with your internal systems, this API can help you reduce operating costs and improve customer service.

The address validation verification feature is an essential part of UPS Shipping and the **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** provides it to its users. ### Why are Residential Rates higher than Commercial Rates?

If you have ever shipped any package using UPS Shipping services, you may be well aware of the fact that UPS charges differently for different address types. Based on the address, UPS may charge you less if the address provided by you is a**Commercial Address**. Whereas, if the address is a **Residential Address** , UPS generally charges more shipping charges.

UPS ships items to two types of locations—**Commercial/Business** and **Residential**. Commercial addresses are generally the locations that are considered commercial real estate. These locations are usually classified as a warehouse that has tractor-trailer access and a loading dock.

Residential addresses, on the other hand, are any business location that is operated from a home, apartment, or any other similar area where people live. Delivery trucks usually enter these areas to perform the final delivery. In addition, the overall shipping charges are slightly higher than that of commercial addresses.

### Would the WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin automatically make city and state appear in the address field by entering only the Zip code?

**No.**

Any address can be recognized as Commercial/Residential only when the ‘Street Address’ line details are provided on the checkout page. WooCommerce, by default, does not provide an option to enter Address Street Lines on the Cart page.

Additionally, the Cart and Checkout pages are provided by WooCommerce itself. **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** will only display the rates and services received from UPS API. So the plugin does not have any control over these fields and is completely handled by WooCommerce and the theme used by the store owner.
