In this article, we will show how you can adjust the shipping cost to include a few extra costs to your UPS LTL Freight shipments when using the **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin with Print Label](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)**.

For those customers who are involved in the business including heavy items, **[UPS Freight](https://www.pluginhive.com/ups-freight-a-guide-for-woocommerce-users/)** is one of the most reliable shipping options. However, most of the time, heavy products require more heavy packaging material to avoid any damage to the product. Online store owners do not have other option but to include such materials in the package. In doing so, the weight of the package increases. Also, they have to bear the cost of this additional material. So, in this article, we will see how WooCommerce store owners can adjust their shipping costs to include this extra cost.

According to Nicolas, a WooCommerce store owner who deals in freight shipments, “ _**We do a lot of LTL Freight shipping. Our major concern is, because of the nature of the large shipments, we require pallets or special padding. We want to be able to add the cost of that in the shipping cost.**_

_**A pallet, for example, costs 13$. So I want to be able to add that to the cost of shipping.**_ “

* * *

## Adding Additional Cost to Shipping

[**WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/) is one of the best shipping plugins. Using it Price Adjustment option WooCommerce store owners can easily add the extra handling cost to the shipping cost. However, the WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin provides two ways of doing that.

  * **By Adding a Flat Rate**
  * **By Adding a Percentage Cost**
