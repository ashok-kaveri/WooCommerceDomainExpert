# Accept Payments in Multiple Currencies for your WooCommerce Bookings

If you are a WooCommerce store owner, you might have noticed that WooCommerce supports a single currency by default. For a growing business, it’s not ideal to accept payments in just one currency. It narrows down the volume of the total potential customers thus, making multiple currencies support a must.

There are a lot of competent plugins out there that allows you to accept payments in multiple currencies. But here, we’ll check out the WooCommerce Multi-Currency plugin – one of the widely used plugins for multiple currency support for your WooCommerce store, and see how it handles your bookings in different currencies.

With this article, we will also check out how you can use **WooCommerce Multi-Currency plugin** with **[WooCommerce Bookings and Appointments plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/)** to make sure your customers can place a booking on your website and pay in their own currencies.

## How does the WooCommerce Multi-Currency plugin work?

The WooCommerce Multi-Currency plugin is nifty little plugin that provides currency switching features and re-calculates the prices everywhere on your store.

Customers can choose the desired currency using the sidebar widget provided by the plugin. They can then see the re-calculated prices and pay in the right currency.

## Setting Up WooCommerce Multiple Currencies on your store

Once you’ve installed the plugin, the process is quite straightforward. For the sake of explanation, we’ve taken the example of the following currencies. Let’s see how you can set up WooCommerce multiple currencies.

  * **INR – Indian Rupee**
  * **USD – United States Dollar**
  * **CAD – Canadian Dollar**
  * **EUR – Euro**

You can configure the plugin for more currencies based on the areas where you provide your services.

### Enabling the WooCommerce Multiple Currencies Selector Widget

Once you’re done with the configuration, don’t forget to set up the WooCommerce Currency Widget and place it at a suitable location on your website.

## How to set up WooCommerce Multiple Currencies for your Bookings store?
