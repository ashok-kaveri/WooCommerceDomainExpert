# WPML Support with WooCommerce Multi Carrier Shipping Plugin

We have some good news! PluginHive has been working with the WPML Team regarding the compatibility of **[WooCommerce Multi-Carrier Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/multiple-carrier-shipping-plugin-woocommerce/)** with the WPML multilingual plugin.

* * *

## How does the WPML integration help PluginHive users?

This new integration will enable you to expand your business globally. You can ship domestically and internationally without worrying about language translations.

It has been a great pleasure working with the WPML team to test the plugin’s compatibility. We ensure that the WooCommerce Multi-Carrier Shipping Plugin works flawlessly with WPML.

* * *

### Why use WPML?

WPML has been the industry standard for more than 500,000 websites when it comes to multilingual translations for plugins, themes, theme options, blog posts, etc. on WordPress. Using this plugin you can provide a comprehensive translation of your store to customers worldwide.

To make your website multi-lingual so that even the Multi-Carrier shipping plugin can work in multiple languages, you would need to purchase the**[WPML Plugin.](https://wpml.org/purchase)**

* * *

### Why use the WooCommerce Multi Carrier Shipping Plugin?

**[WooCommerce Multi-Carrier Shipping Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/multiple-carrier-shipping-plugin-woocommerce/)** by PluginHive is one of the best shipping plugins that provide the integration to not one but multiple shipping carriers. WooCommerce Multi-Carrier Shipping plugin supports shipping carriers like UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL, and Stamps. The plugin provides real-time shipping rates for both domestic as well as international shipments.

You can check out our plugin’s WPML compatibility page and read the documentation: **[WPML Compatibility for WooCommerce Multi-Carrier Shipping Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/using-wpml-woocommerce-multi-carrier-shipping-plugin-multilingual-sites/)**.

If you need any help setting up multiple carriers shipping on your WooCommerce store, feel free to **[Contact PluginHive Customer Support](https://www.pluginhive.com/support/)**. We would be more than happy to help.

_Good luck!_
