# Offer WooCommerce Shipping Options from Different Carriers

WooCommerce provides store owners to offer a variety of products to their customers. Having more choices makes it easy for store owners to create their customer base and grow their business. However, when it comes to shipping, there is not much variety for the customers. Ideally, WooCommerce store owners provide one shipping carrier as they generally might be using only one shipping carrier. This limits the store owners as well as the customers to only one shipping carrier and most probably a few shipping services.

According to Mark, a WooCommerce store owner,” _**…I would like to ask in here for some help for my WooCommerce store. Is there a way to have a dynamic choice of Shipping Carriers when checkout? What I mean is that the visitor should be able to choose shipping options for multiple carriers (USPS, UPS, DHL, FedEx). I currently use 2 different carriers, USPS & UPS. But when checkout I generally see the fees for USPS. Anybody can help me with that..?**_“

In this article, we will be discussing the **[WooComerce Multi-Carrier Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/multiple-carrier-shipping-plugin-woocommerce/)** and how using this plugin, store owners can easily allow their customers to have a choice of shipping services from various shipping carriers.

* * *

## Showing Shipping Options from Multiple Shipping Carriers on the Cart Page

WooCommerce Multi-Carrier Shipping plugin allows store owners to display shipping rates from the following shipping carriers.

  * **UPS**
  * **FedEx**
  * **USPS**
  * **DHL**
  * **Stamps**

Using this plugin, store owners can provide their customers to have shipping options from more than one shipping carrier. The store owners can easily select the shipping carrier from the list of shipping carriers and then choose which shipping service to display on the cart page. Based on Mark’s requirements, if he requires shipping services from UPS and USPS, he only needs to configure some shipping rules for the shipping services that he wants to show to his customers.
