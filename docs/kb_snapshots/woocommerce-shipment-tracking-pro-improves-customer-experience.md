# How Shipment Tracking Pro for WooCommerce improves customer experience?

Tracking your shipments has become an important aspect of online shopping. What eCommerce stores are looking for is to automatically notify customers about their orders as soon as it is shipped, until it reaches their door-step. This way customers no longer have to keep checking up with the store owners about the tracking status of their orders. **[PH Shipment Tracking Pro for WooCommerce](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-shipment-tracking-pro/)** is the perfect WooCommerce shipping solution that does exactly this. This simple yet powerful WordPress plugin simplifies shipment tracking on your WooCommerce store and makes it easy for your customers to track their orders.

Let us check out how PH Shipment Tracking Pro for WooCommerce makes it easier for customers to know the whereabouts of their shipment in the following 5 ways.

  * Shipment tracking details are sent to the customers via WooCommerce Order Completion Email
  * Customers can view shipment tracking status on the tracking page
  * Customers can log in and view shipment tracking status on My Account > Orders page
  * Email notifications are sent to the customers on every shipment tracking status update by the carrier
  * WooCommerce store owners can view the shipment tracking details and follow up to avoid delivery delays

* * *

### Email notifications are sent to the customers on every shipment tracking status update by the carrier

One of the best features of the PH Shipment Tracking Pro for WooCommerce is that the plugin notifies the customers via email notifications as soon as the shipping carriers update the tracking status of the shipment.

The plugin will send email notifications to the customers on the following shipment tracking status updates.

  * In transit
  * Our for delivery
  * Delivered
