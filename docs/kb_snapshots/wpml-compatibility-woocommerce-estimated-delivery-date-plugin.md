# WooCommerce Estimated Delivery Date plugin with WPML Support

We have some good news! PluginHive has been working with the WPML Team regarding the compatibility of the **[WooCommerce Estimated Delivery Date plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/estimated-delivery-date-plugin-woocommerce/)** with the WPML multilingual plugin.

## How does the WPML integration help PluginHive users?

This new integration will enable you to expand your business globally and provide your customers with a better experience domestically and internationally without worrying about language translations.

It has been a great pleasure working with the WPML team to conduct the compatibility testing of the plugin to make sure that the **[WooCommerce Estimated Delivery Date Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/estimated-delivery-date-plugin-woocommerce/)** works flawlessly with WPML.

* * *

### Why use WPML?

WPML has been the industry standard for more than 500,000 websites when it comes to multi-lingual translations for plugins, themes, theme options, blog posts, etc. on WordPress. Using this plugin you can provide a comprehensive translation of your store to customers all around the world.

In order to make your website multi-lingual so that even the Estimated Delivery Date plugin can work in multiple languages, you would need to purchase the**[WPML Plugin](https://wpml.org/purchase).**

* * *

### Why use WooCommerce Estimated Delivery Date Plugin?

**[WooCommerce Estimated Delivery Date plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/estimated-delivery-date-plugin-woocommerce/)** by PluginHive is one of the best delivery estimation plugins that provide the delivery dates of the products based on the store owner’s requirement. It allows WooCommerce store owners to set up a delivery estimate for their products based on the type of product as well as the location where the products are being shipped.

You can check out the WPML compatibility page for our plugin and also read the documentation for **[WPML compatibility for WooCommerce Estimated Delivery Date Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/using-wpml-woocommerce-estimated-delivery-date-plugin-multilingual-sites/)**.

If you need any help setting up product estimated delivery dates on your WooCommerce store, feel free to **[Contact PluginHive Customer Support](https://www.pluginhive.com/support/)**. We would be more than happy to help.

_Good luck!_
