# Flat Rate Shipping based on Product Quantity – WooCommerce Multi Carrier plugin

In this article, we will be discussing the **[WooCommerce Multi Carrier Shippin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/multiple-carrier-shipping-plugin-woocommerce/)** g plugin. This article will cover how store owners can simplify shipping in case of multiple products by providing a flat rate shipping charge for multiple quantities of any product.

WooCommerce store owners deal with every type of shipping scenario. Be it shipping a small product or multiple products with a large weight and size. Based on the product quantity, sometimes the store owners may even need to make sure to charge appropriate shipping from their customers too. According to Ryan, an online WooCommerce store owner, “ _**… Hi..! Is there any way to set up a flat rate for shipping multiple of products? For example:**_

_**I want to charge $10 for 1-10 items of any mix. So whether they order 1 item or 10 items, the shipping fee will be $10. And I want to charge $12 for 11-30 items of any mix. So whether they order 11 items or 30 items, the shipping fee will be $12… I appreciate your help… Thanks**_ “

## Creating Shipping Rules for Flat Rate Shipping based on Product Quantity

WooCommerce Multi-Carrier Shipping plugin supports multiple shipping carriers as well as conditional shipping rules for all the shipping carriers that it supports as well as for Flat Rate shipping. According to Ryan, since he requires shipping rules based on product quantity, this can be easily configured in the plugin settings. All Ryan needs to do is to select the Flat Rate option from the list and configure quantity-based shipping. ## Summary…

In this article, we discussed the WooCommerce Multi-Carrier Shipping plugin and how with the help of this plugin WooCommerce store owners can easily set up shipping rules based on multiple quantities of products. With the help of this article, WooCommerce store owners can create shipping rules based on the quantity range and offer customers a flat rate shipping. This way the customers will have the comfort of buying more products since the minimum, as well as the maximum quantity range, will have similar shipping rates. So the customers won’t have to pay extra shipping charges to buy more. This way it will benefit both customers as well as the store owners.
