# WooCommerce Bookings Email Notification

In this article, we’ll discuss the emails sent during the booking process by the **[Bookings And Appointments for WooCommerce](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/)** and we’ll show you how to set up WooCommerce Follow up emails and WooCommerce Reminder emails for your bookings.

## Why do you need to send emails to your customers?

Timely reminders, follow-ups, and notifications play important roles in ensuring a great pre-booking and post-booking experience. These emails connect you to your customers in a much more natural way and keep them updated.

With the [**Bookings And Appointments for WooCommerce**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/), you can ensure your customers will never miss their appointments or bookings since the plugin sends email reminders before the appointment.

Read along as we uncover some of the most convenient ways to send email notifications to your customers using the Bookings And Appointments for WooCommerce.

## WooCommerce Bookings Confirmation/Cancellation Email

[**Bookings And Appointments for WooCommerce**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/) allow customers to request a booking and send it to the Admin for confirmation. Based on the store availability or the preparations, the Admin can either accept the booking or reject it. In either case, **the plugin sends customers an email stating the booking status**.

### Email for booking awaiting confirmation

This email is sent to the customers after they place the booking that requires confirmation from the store owner.

* * *

#### How to Customize the Booking Awaiting Confirmation Email

Follow the steps below to customize the booking awaiting the confirmation email that is sent to the customers.

  * Navigate to **WooCommerce - > Settings -> Emails**
  * Visit the “**PH Booking Requires Confirmation** ” email notification and click on **Manage**
  * Click on the “**Copy file to theme** ” button on the bottom-right side
  * Click on the “**View Template** ” button
  * This will display the email editor window where you can customize the email content
  * Once you have customized the email as per your preference, click on **Save Changes**

* * *

## **Send Reminder and Follow-up Emails to Customers**

Keeping customers informed before and after their booking helps reduce no-shows and gives your service a more personal, professional feel. The[ Bookings and Appointments for WooCommerce](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/) plugin lets you send two types of automated emails: a **reminder email** before the booking begins, and a **follow-up email** after it’s completed.

Both are configured under **Bookings → Settings → Reminder and Follow up Emails** tab.

* * *

### **Reminder Emails — Sent Before the Booking**

**To set it up:**

  1. Visit **Bookings → Settings → Reminder and Follow up Emails** tab.
  2. Enable **Booking Reminder Emails**.
  3. **Select the Email Template** – choose between **Plugin Default** or **WooCommerce Emails** styling.
  4. Set your **Reminder Email Subject**.
  5. Customize the **Reminder Email Content** using the merge tags below.
  6. Set **“Enter how many minutes in advance the reminder email should be sent”** – for example, 1444 minutes sends the reminder roughly a day before the booking starts.

**Available merge tags:**

Tag| Adds  
---|---  
[CUSTOMER_NAME]| The customer’s name  
[SITE_NAME]| Your website/store name  
[BOOKING_DETAILS]| Booking details – product name, duration, cost, etc.  
  
* * *

* * *

Click **Save changes** once you’re done.

This is how the reminder email will appear to the customer before their booking.

* * *

### **Follow-Up Emails – Sent After the Booking Is Completed**

A follow-up email is sent after a booking is marked **Completed** – a good touchpoint for thanking the customer or asking for a review.

**To set it up:**

  1. In the same **Reminder and Follow up Emails** tab, enable **Enable Follow up Emails**.
  2. **Select the Email Template** – choose between **Plugin Default** or **WooCommerce Emails** styling.
  3. Set your **Follow up Email Subject**.
  4. Customize the **Follow up Email Content** , using the same merge tags ([CUSTOMER_NAME], [SITE_NAME], [BOOKING_DETAILS]) as above.
  5. Set the **Follow up delay time (Minutes)** – how long to wait, in minutes, after the booking is marked Completed before sending the email.

* * *

Click **Save changes** once you’re done.

This is how the follow-up email will appear to the customer after their booking is completed.

* * *

### **How Emails Are Scheduled**

Reminder and follow-up emails are scheduled individually for each booking as soon as it’s created, so they go out reliably at the intended time – including for stores running in a non-UTC time zone.

**Troubleshooting:** If you need to check whether a reminder or follow-up email fired correctly for a specific booking, visit **Bookings → Settings → Diagnostics** tab and enable the **Reminder Email Debug Log** or **Follow-up Email Debug Log** toggle. Each writes to its own log file, viewable at **WooCommerce → Status → Logs**.
