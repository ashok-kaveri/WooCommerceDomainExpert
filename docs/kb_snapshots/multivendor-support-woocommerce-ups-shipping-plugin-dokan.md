# Dokan Integration with WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin

As we enter April, a piece of great news is ready to kick off. Our team has been working on the native support for the **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** with the Dokan Multivendor plugin. And we are glad to announce that our plugin is fully compatible with their plugin.

Any WooCommerce store that offers multiple items from multiple vendors is bound to easily generate healthy sales and traffic. Having multiple vendors also reduces the stress of shipping and product management. So, a multivendor website is a win-win for you and your vendors.

Dokan is one of the best and most popular [WooCommerce multivendor plugins](https://www.pluginhive.com/woocommerce-multi-vendor-plugins/). By using this plugin, your vendors will get to enjoy the features and services that are not available anywhere else. The UPS Shipping plugin, on the other hand, is an absolute delight when it comes to logistics management. Both you and your vendors can manage order deliveries and print the shipping labels directly from the admin side.

* * *

* * *

If you want anyone of your visitors to become your vendor then you can enable an option in the plugin settings page. Once you enable this option, anyone can register themselves as a vendor in your store.

With this combination, you will be highly efficient in streamlining the necessary steps required to fulfill an order. And using the WooCommerce UPS plugin in a multivendor environment is as easy as it can get. All you need is to follow a few simple steps:

  1. Install and configure the [Dokan](https://dokan.co/wordpress/). The following document will help you get your site ready in no time: [Dokan Multi vendor-Getting Started](https://dokan.co/docs/wordpress/getting-started/).
  2. In  _**WooCommerce** > **Dokan** > **Settings,**_ you would be able to configure the plugin based on your needs. The following document should help configure both the plugins.
  3. Install the [WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/) from the PluginHive page. If you are already running it, make sure that you update it to the latest version (3.9.12 or newer).
  4. Verify that the UPS Shipping plugin works. You can do that by checking if the shipping rates appear on the Cart and Checkout page.
  5. **You are ready!**

So, that’s about it. With this compatibility and combination of the two plugins, you can change the way you deal with your customers. This solution will ultimately help you increase both your sales and potential buyers.
