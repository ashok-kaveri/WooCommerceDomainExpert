## How can Customers collect their packages from a UPS Access Point?

Judy owns an online**Art Gallery, based in California, U.S.A.** Being a small business she currently ships across the country. Hence she has chosen UPS Shipping to help her deliver shipments to her customers. She uses shipping methods like **Next Day Air, 2nd Day Air, 3 Day Select,** etc. as she wants faster delivery for her customers.

But it wasn’t long before she started facing issues regarding customers opting for refunds because their shipments were not getting delivered on time. On checking, she found that the delivery was on time but the delivery timing and the work hours of most of her customers were clashing. As a result, either customers were not able to receive their packages at the address that they mentioned, or the UPS drivers were not able to reach customers at their addresses.

What Judy needs is a way for her customers can select a nearby location or a store where the package could be delivered based on their preference. Besides, that location would have to be secure and authenticated by UPS itself.

* * *

## What is UPS Access Point?

The idea behind **UPS Access Point** is to give store owners an additional option to deliver the shipment especially when they are not comfortable with the delivery timings. Additionally, it also serves as a great option for customers to have a trusted place to take care of their orders.

Here are some of the highlights of the Access Point service by UPS:

  * Serves as a convenient option for store owners to deliver on time.
  * Provides customers with an option to collect their orders on their behalf.
  * Has an availability of **lockers** so that the package remains secure and doesn’t get mixed up with other person’s package.
  * Most of the Access Points are open 24×7.
  * Customers are required to produce a valid ID to collect the package from the Access Point.

After looking at these highlights, there is no doubt that this UPS Service has not left any margin for error when it comes to shipment security.

* * *

## What is UPS Access Point Economy service?

The UPS Access Point Economy service is like any other shipping service except that it ships to and from Access Points within the same country. These locations are an integral part of the wide UPS network that deals mostly with independently-owned businesses. This service offers a convenient and safe way to drop off and pick up of your packages.

With this contractual shipping service, you can save money while shipping and receiving returns directly from your business to Access Point locations within the same country. UPS’s network includes locations with weekend and evening hours to fit your customers’ schedules.

## How to Collect your package from a UPS Access Point?

Since we already discussed how secure the UPS Access Point service is, collecting the package from there will have the same level of sophistication attached to it.

The package can be easily tracked by UPS Tracking services using the Tracking Number. That tracking number is either sent to the customers automatically or they can contact the store owners for tracking details. And as soon as the package arrives at the UPS Access Point, customers can collect it using the **UPS Package Tracking Number**.

Since these Access Points are open 24×7, customers can collect the package at any moment. Sometimes the authorized personnel can ask the customers to produce **valid IDs** , such as a driving license or any other document for verification. Other than that it is pretty convenient for customers to collect their package.
