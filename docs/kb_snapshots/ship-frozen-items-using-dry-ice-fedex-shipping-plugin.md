## Why shipping with Dry Ice is considered difficult

Shipping with dry ice is one of the reasons why many shipping carriers charge extra. But the problem doesn’t end here. Most store owners aren’t aware of the shipping process that involves dry ice. Dry ice shipping through FedEx is allowed only for a few shipping services. It is also considered a dangerous and hazardous material, which puts a store owner in a much more difficult situation. 

However, following the right set of procedures will make the shipping process more straightforward and much easier. You can further save the extra charges spent on shipping by following the right steps to ship the frozen items.

## What is the best way to prepare your package and ship frozen items?

Dry Ice is the solid form of carbon dioxide. The good thing about using dry ice is that it has a lower temperature than that of water ice. It acts as the cooling agent when you want to ship items that are sensitive to heat. Moreover, it does not leave any residue, which is the most important part of shipping consumables and delicate frozen items.

#### **Packing and preparing your shipments**

There are a few packaging methods that are necessary to do before shipping your perishable items. FedEx has recommended the following packing procedure for packing perishable items with dry ice.

  1. You must ensure that your packaging allows the release of pressure from within the package. This is because the dry ice releases carbon dioxide gas which can build up enough space to rupture the packaging. For example, you should not use steel drums or jerricans as outer packaging. You should also not place dry ice in sealed plastic bags.
  2. You should use good quality fiberboard corrugated cardboard), plastic, or wooden boxes.
  3. Use a layer of Styrofoam inside the box for insulation, and make sure you do not seal it airtight.
  4. Do not use Styrofoam as outer packaging unless FedEx Packaging Services has approved it. Also, keep the dry ice amount within the 200 kg limit per package.

Now, you need to wrap the frozen item with plastic wrap prior to packing. Make sure you pack the frozen item and the dry ice tightly together. Use newspaper to keep everything secure and properly packed. Doing this will provide extra insulation which will, in turn, keep your perishables and frozen food fresh.

#### **Dry Ice Labelling Requirements**

Another important thing to note is that you have to signify that your parcel says that the shipment contains dry ice. If you look at the image shown above, you would notice that the shipment package has a label that says **UN 1845**. This code is necessary as per the **International Air Transport Association (IATA)** dangerous goods regulations. You have to mention the following markings on the outer packaging of all IATA dry ice shipments:

1.”**Dry Ice** ” or “**Carbon Dioxide Solid** ” along with the code **UN 1845** should be present.

  2. Net weight of dry ice should be displayed in kilograms.
  3. Name and address of the shipper and recipient should be clearly mentioned on the outer box area.
