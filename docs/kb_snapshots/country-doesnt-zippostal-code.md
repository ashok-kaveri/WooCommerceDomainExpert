# What to do when my country doesn’t have a zip/postal code?

In this small guide, we will tell you what to do when your country does not have a ZIP or Postal code. This guide should help you when using the **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)**.

**Customer:**

Hi, I am from the United Arab Emirates. Zip codes are not used in our country. How do I go about getting rates on the UPS plugin?

* * *

**PluginHive Support:**

We have this functionality available in the [**WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/), which replaces the postal code field with the city field. This will be useful when you are shipping from a country that doesn’t have a postal code.

To show the shipping options for the United Arab Emirates, you need to provide the city with information in the specified field. Once you enter the name of the city in the city field, prices will be reflected.

If you need any help setting up UPS shipping on your WooCommerce store, feel free to **[Contact PluginHive Customer Support](https://www.pluginhive.com/support/)**. We would be more than happy to help.

_Good luck!_
