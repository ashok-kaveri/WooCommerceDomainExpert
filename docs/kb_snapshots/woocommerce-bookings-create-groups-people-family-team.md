# Accept WooCommerce Bookings in Groups of People, Families & Team Members

With this article, we’ll show you how to accept WooCommerce bookings in groups of people, families, and team members using the **[Bookings And Appointments for WooCommerce](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/)** plugin.

## How to Create Groups Using the WooCommerce Bookings plugin?

By default, the **[Bookings And Appointments for WooCommerce](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/)** lets define participants that allow you to get the number of guests. You can also use this data to calculate the total booking cost.

With the help of a special addon, you can combine the number of participants and create multiple groups with a pre-defined value. This way you can set up a group of 4 family members or a group of 12 people.

* * *

## WooCommerce Booking Participants Groups Addon

**With the WooCommerce Bookings Participants Groups Addon** along with the help of the **PluginHive Bookings And Appointments for WooCommerce plugin** , you can set up a group based on an existing participant. Thereafter, you can assign the number of group members to that group. The group member count will determine how many slots a participant would take while placing a booking.

### Benefits of using the WooCommerce Booking Participants Groups Addon

The **WooCommerce Booking Participant Groups Addon** is the perfect tool for adventure package bookings where you could offer booking based on a team of people.

You have to predefine the number of people in a team so that the customers can book accordingly. Similarly, the booking cost would be calculated based on the units selected while booking.

Most importantly, the Booking Participant Groups add-on accurately calculates the remaining booking slots based on the number of people already who have already booked. All you need to do is create a Group and set the number of people allowed in that group.

## How to use WooCommerce Booking Participants Groups Addon?

Let’s consider an online tour booking business where a family of four members has to be considered as one participant.

**Business owner:**_While setting up ticket booking using the Bookings And Appointments for WooCommerce plugin, I want the customers to choose how many families will be traveling when it comes to a single booking. The Family option will be considered as 4 members; hence, 4 tickets will be booked for a single order._
