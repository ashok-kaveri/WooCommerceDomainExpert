## Navigating the Euro as the Universal Currency

The Euro, serving as the de facto universal currency for European e-commerce, offers a multitude of advantages to businesses looking to expand beyond their local borders. Its adoption simplifies the complexities of managing multiple currencies while streamlining cross-border transactions. Here’s a deeper dive into this pivotal aspect:

**Advantages of Euro Adoption**

  * European Union widely accepts and recognizes the Euro, creating a sense of familiarity and trust among customers.
  * It simplifies pricing, allowing businesses to display product prices uniformly in a single currency, enhancing transparency for international buyers.
  * Reduces the complexity of financial management, making accounting and financial reporting more straightforward for businesses operating in multiple European countries.
  * The Euro facilitates market entry into various European countries, allowing businesses to tap into a broader customer base without the hassle of handling multiple local currencies.

### WooCommerce Currency Conversion for FedEx Shipping Rates

  * In order to convert the currency to Euro across the whole WooCommerce store, you need to navigate to **WooCommerce > Settings > General** and set the WooCommerce currency to Euro, as shown below.

  * Now, for WooCommerce shipping currency conversion, you need to visit the **[WooCommerce FedEx Shipping plugin’s](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-fedex-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** settings page. Navigate to **WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping > FedEx > Rates & Services** and navigate to the FedEx currency option.

**FedEx Currency** will be the currency in which the **[FedEx account](https://www.fedex.com/en-au/shipping/industry-solutions/ecommerce/alliances-pluginhive.html)** provides shipping costs. The currency varies from country to country. When you use FedEx’s services to ship a package, the currency used for billing will be the currency of the country where the shipment originates. In this case, we have the shipment originating from the UK, and hence the FedEx currency is Pound Sterling.

  * In order to get accurate shipping rates in the Euro, add the currency **conversion rate from the Euro to Pound Sterling, i.e. 0.86** , in the **Conversion Rate** field.

  * After saving the settings, the plugin will display accurate shipping rates on the WooCommerce cart and checkout page. In the image below you can compare and test the FedEx shipping rates between the WooCommerce cart page and the FedEx official website.

FedEx shipping cost for a 5kg parcel from the UK to Italy:

  * **£172.38 with FedEx International Priority**
  * **£123.16 with FedEx International Economy**

After the currency conversion within the WooCommerce store, the cost is displayed on the WooCommerce cart & checkout page:

  * **€199.61 with FedEx International Priority**
  * **€142.61 with FedEx International Economy**

## Impact of WooCommerce Currency Conversion on Shipping

Accurate currency conversion is not just a financial matter; it significantly influences shipping rate calculation within WooCommerce. Accurate currency conversion is essential for calculating precise shipping rates, especially when customers from various regions are placing orders. When customers view shipping rates in their preferred currency, it enhances transparency, reducing the likelihood of **[cart abandonment](https://www.pluginhive.com/woocommerce-abandon-cart-plugins/)**.

Inaccurate currency conversion can lead to discrepancies between displayed shipping rates and actual shipping costs. This can result in undercharging or overcharging customers, leading to dissatisfaction and potential financial losses.

The choice of currency also affects tax and duty calculations on the shipping costs, making it crucial to make these calculations accurately.
