# Sell both WooCommerce Digital products and Physical goods

With this guide, we’ll tell you the best way to sell and ship both WooCommerce Digital products and Physical goods. We’ll use the **[WooCommerce Canada Post Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-canada-post-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** along with WooCommerce Dynamic Pricing and Discounts plugin in order to do so.

* * *

## What are WooCommerce Digital Products?

WooCommerce digital products are nothing but items that are sold over the internet without having the need to manage them physically. These products are available to download from websites and/or shared via email or similar methods.

Digital products include items like audio/song files, photos, e-books, PDF or [docs](https://www.canva.com/docs/) files, zip files, videos, games, software keys, etc., They don’t require stock management and are easy to set up.

* * *

## How to Sell WooCommerce Digital Products and Physical Goods?

Before we begin, you need to understand how digital products are processed by WooCommerce. Check out [this guide](https://docs.woocommerce.com/document/digital-downloadable-product-handling/) for that.

For the sake of understanding, let’s consider the following real-life example.

John is from Canada and is keen on starting an online education portal. He has a list of courses in **Web Designing** and decides to use WordPress as his CMS(Content Management System). Once the course is enrolled by a customer, he plans to send/ship a hard copy of the respective reference book.

John can achieve the solution by creating two separate WooCommerce products – a digital and another physical.

* * *

### WooCommerce Plugins required

As a first step, John has to create a virtual product with the course name. He will be selling this item as downloadable content.

Next, to ship the book with every order, he needs to attach the shipping label and then proceed with the shipping.

John figures out that he can fulfill the order using a dynamic pricing plugin and any of these [**WooCommerce Shipping Plugins**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product-category/woocommerce-plugin/woocommerce-shipping/).

  1. **[WooCommerce Canada Post Shipping Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-canada-post-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)**(since John lives in Canada)
  2. **Dynamic Pricing and Discounts Plugin for WooCommerce**
