# WPML Compatibility with WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin

Great news for all the WooCommerce store owners who want to expand their business throughout the world. Over the last few days, we have been working alongside the WPML team to ensure **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** ‘s compatibility with the WPML plugin.

* * *

## How does the WPML integration help PluginHive users?

This new integration will enable you to expand your business globally and ship domestically and internationally without worrying about language translations.

It has been a great pleasure working with the WPML team to conduct the compatibility testing of the plugin to make sure that the **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** works flawlessly with WPML.

* * *

### Why use WPML?

WPML has been the industry standard for more than 500,000 websites when it comes to multi-lingual translations for plugins, themes, theme options, blog posts, etc. on WordPress. Using this plugin you can provide a comprehensive translation of your store to customers all around the world.

In order to make your website multi-lingual so that even the UPS shipping rates are shown in multiple languages, you would need to purchase the**[WPML Plugin](https://wpml.org/purchase).**

### Why use the WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin?

On the other hand, WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin by PluginHive is the best UPS shipping solution for WooCommerce users. The plugin provides real-time shipping rates from UPS for both domestic as well as international shipments. It is among the only plugins that support an end-to-end automated shipment creation from placing an order till getting the shipping labels as well as the tracking details for the shipments.

You can check out the WPML compatibility page for our plugin and also read the documentation: **[WPML compatibility for WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/using-wpml-woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-multilingual-sites/).**

If you need any help setting up UPS shipping on your WooCommerce store, feel free to **[Contact PluginHive Customer Support](https://www.pluginhive.com/support/)**. We would be more than happy to help.

_Good luck!_
