# How to Reserve Meeting Rooms with Bookings and Appointments for WooCommerce plugin

In this guide, we will tell you how to set up an online meeting room reservation system using the **[Bookings and Appointments for WooCommerce plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-booking-and-appointments/). **We will take a real-life example to help you understand how it’s done.

## The Business Case

The Bookings and Appointments for WooCommerce plugin is a very capable bookings plugin that can be used with almost any business that requires a booking system. Let’s go through a business case and see how this plugin can help.

  1. Customers will select the date and time for the meeting room, where each booking slot consists of 30 minutes. Customers can select multiple booking slots if he/she wants a meeting room for more than 30 minutes. The meeting room is available from 6 AM – 11 PM every day.
  2. The system should also show whichever slots are available or not available during the day.
  3. 30-minute buffer after every booking for the meeting room to be cleaned for the next booking.
  4. The booking cost is $100 per block (or 30 minutes).

* * *

## Solution using **Bookings and Appointments for WooCommerce**

  * Create a product called “Meeting Room”

* * *

  * Set the booking period as a calendar range of 30 minutes.

* * *

  * Provide the availability option accordingly, that is from 6 AM to 11 PM every day.

* * *

  * Provide a buffer time of 30 minutes after every booking.

* * *

  * Set the cost per block to $100.

* * *

  * And voila! Your customers will now be able to book a meeting room for say, 2 hours, from 9 AM – 11 AM. Have a look at the image below. 

* * *

  * And the booking calendar will also display the availability and non-availability dates within the calendar for booking.

* * *

  * Once booked, 30 minutes buffer time will automatically be assigned after every booking by the plugin.

* * *
