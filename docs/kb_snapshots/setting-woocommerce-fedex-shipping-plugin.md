**If you select Help Desk:**

This method requires you to contact your **FedEx Account Manager or FedEx Tech Support** and request a **bypass for FedEx REST registration**.

Before reaching out, keep the following details ready:

  * FedEx Account Number
  * FedEx Billing Address (including Email ID and Phone Number)
  * Recent FedEx Invoice ID

Once the FedEx team confirms the bypass, return to the plugin and select the **Help Desk** option for account validation. A confirmation screen will appear asking whether you’ve already completed the manual validation with the FedEx support team:

  * Click **Proceed** if the bypass has been completed; your account will be registered successfully.
  * Click **Cancel** if you want to go back and choose a different validation method.

* * *

**Important:**   
  
If the bypass has not been completed on the FedEx side, the registration will fail. In that case, reconnect with your FedEx Account Manager or Tech Support and confirm the bypass. For best results, complete the Help Desk registration while still on the call with the FedEx team, so you can immediately verify that the process works.   
  
Alternatively, you can share the required details with the plugin support team, who can coordinate with FedEx on your behalf.

## 5\. Verify your Product Settings

### Set the Product weight and dimensions of the unit

To calculate shipping costs, FedEx requires the weight and dimensions of the product. Our **[FedEx Shipping Plugin for WooCommerce with Print Label](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-fedex-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** offers two unit standards for inputting this information: Pounds and Inches, as well as Kilograms and Centimeters. Once you select your preferred unit standard, the weight and dimensions will be passed to FedEx accordingly. Please refer to the settings shown below to choose your preferred unit standard.

**Accessing Weight/Dimensions Units:**

  * Navigate to _**WooCommerce - > Settings -> Shipping -> FedEx**_
  * Visit the **General Tab - > Weight / Dimensions Units** and set the weight and dimension units as per your country or business requirements

* * *

* * *

Please note that it is mandatory to assign the unit to get accurate rates.

### Add Product Weight and Dimensions

Every product added to your WooCommerce store must be assigned with corresponding weight and dimensions. The values entered are important for multiple functions, such as allocating suitable boxes for shipping, displaying real-time rates on cart/checkout, etc.

Accessing Product Weight and Dimensions

  * Navigate to _**WooCommerce Dashboard** -> **Products**_

  * Select the Product of your choice

* * *

* * *

  * Click on the **Edit Product** option and visit _**Product Data - > Shipping Tab**_
  * Enter the Product **Weight** and **Dimensions** as shown in the image

* * *

* * *

Under **FedEx Shipping Details** , you can configure the following product-specific shipping information:

  * **HS Tariff Number** – Standardized code used to classify the product for customs. This always overrides the default HS code given in the global configuration.
  * **Commodity Description** – Product description used on customs and commercial invoice documents.
  * **Unit of Measure** – Unit used for the commodity quantity on customs declarations. Available options include Default, Carat (AR), Centimeter (CM), Cubic Foot (CFT), Cubic Meter (M3), Dozen (DOZ), Each (EA), Foot (LFT), Gram (G), Kilogram (KG), Liter (LTR), Meter (M), Ounce (OZ), Pair (PR), Pieces (PCS), Pound (LB), and more.
  * **Country of Manufacture** – Country code where the product was manufactured.
  * **Dry Ice** – Mark products that require dry ice for temperature-controlled shipping. This option is visible only when enabled in the plugin’s global settings. The product-level setting always overrides the global configuration.
  * **Freight Class** – Specify the freight classification of the product for LTL (Less-Than-Truckload) shipments. 
  * **Alcohol** – Mark products that contain alcohol and require special handling during shipping. 
  * **Delivery Signature** – Override the store-wide signature requirement for this specific product.
  * **Pre-Packed Product** – Mark the product as already packed in its own shipping-ready packaging.
  * **Non-Standard Product Mark** – Products that require special handling due to unusual size, shape, weight, or packaging.
  * **Custom Declared Value** – Declared value of the product for customs or insurance purposes.

You can also configure the following special shipping options for the product:

  * **Dangerous Goods Type** – Mark products that require hazardous material handling (such as chemicals or gases). 
  * **Battery Product** – Choose whether the product contains a battery. 

These options are also explained later under **Special Services**.

Please enter valid **Country Code of Manufacture** under FedEx Shipping Details

### Obtain FedEx One Rate on Cart/Checkout

FedEx One Rate is a flat-rate shipping service that lets you ship packages up to 50 lbs without calculating shipping costs based on weight or dimensions. As long as the shipment fits within an eligible FedEx One Rate package, you can add items to the package without increasing the shipping cost.

**_Note:_**   
  
FedEx One Rate rates are available only for eligible U.S. domestic shipments packed in supported FedEx One Rate packaging. If the shipment does not qualify, the plugin will use standard FedEx packaging and rate calculations.   
  

* * *

To enable FedEx One Rate:

  1. Go to **WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping > FedEx > Rates & Services**
  2. Enable the **FedEx One Rate** option

* * *

Once enabled, customers will see only the FedEx services that support One Rate pricing at checkout.

Supported FedEx One Rate packaging includes:

  * FedEx Envelope
  * FedEx Pak
  * FedEx Extra Small Box
  * FedEx Small Box
  * FedEx Medium Box
  * FedEx Large Box
  * FedEx Extra Large Box
  * FedEx Tube

* * *

**Enable FedEx One Rate Per Order**

In addition to the global FedEx One Rate setting, the plugin allows you to enable or disable FedEx One Rate for individual orders from the **Edit Order** page.

This lets you override the store-wide setting for a specific eligible U.S. domestic shipment packed in FedEx One Rate packaging. The selected option applies to both shipping rate calculation and shipping label generation, giving you greater flexibility when processing orders.

To get more information about FedEx One Rate Service and corresponding rates, _see_ the [FedEx One Rate site](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/save-shipping-cost-using-fedex-one-rate/). After going to the website, click the Get Rates button to get rates for various services.

* * *

**_Note:_**   
  
All FedEx boxes are not available for all countries, disable this option or disable different boxes if you are not receiving any shipping services.

### Add FedEx Insurance Value

Every Shipment is transported on a limited liability basis as provided by FedEx. If Shipper requires greater protection, then insurance may be arranged at an additional cost by FedEx. The setting is as shown below:

Accessing Insurance Option: 

  * Select **WooCommerce < Settings < Shipping < FedEx**
  * Select **General**
  * Enable the **Third Party Insurance** Option to set Insurance for your package.
  * Additionally, you can set a **Minimum Order Amount**(e.g., $100) to ensure insurance is only applied to qualifying shipments, helping control unnecessary insurance costs.

### Selecting the Rate type

FedEx provides different rates, which depend on whether the customer is an account holder or not. The rate-type setting is as given below:

The available options are as given below:

  * List Rate: FedEx provides standard shipping rates for the public. Select this option if you want to offer only standard rates.
  * Account Rate: FedEx provides a discounted rate to FedEx Account holders. Select this option if you want to facilitate the customer at a discounted rate.

### Enable Shipping Taxes on Cart/Checkout

Shipment may incur fees in addition to the base shipping rate, depending on the service options you choose. Using the Shipping Quote type setting, we can include and exclude fees along with the base shipping rate:

The quote type options supported are listed below:

  * **Base Shipping Cost:** Shipping without any discounts, taxes, and Surcharges. When this feature is enabled, the rates fetched from the FedEx API are shown without any addition of taxes.
  * **Total net shipping cost without Tax:** Shipping Cost with discount and surcharges. When this feature is enabled, the prices will be discounted and extra surcharges will be added to the rates displayed for the shipment services
  * **Total Net Shipping Cost:** Shipping Cost with discount, surcharges & transportation taxes. When this feature is enabled in the rates and services tab, the shipment cost is discounted and extra surcharges and transportation taxes are added.
  * **Total Net Shipping Cost with Duties & Taxes: **Cost with discount surcharges, transportation taxes & all other international taxes. When this service is enabled in the rates and services section shipment services costs are discounted and extra surcharges are added and transportation charges and other international taxes are added. 

### Choosing the Offer Rates

Once you get the quotes from FedEx, you can choose which quotes to show to the Customer. The setting is as shown below:

You can select the required option from the Offer Rates drop-down list and the options available are as given below:

  * Offer the Customer all the return rates: This option shows quotes for all the available services, which are returned by FedEx and also selected by you in step 2.
  * Offer the Customer the cheapest rate only, anonymously: This option shows the only service with the cheapest quote from the available services.

### Add Currency and Conversion rate options

  * **FedEx Currency** : Specify which currency is being used for your FedEx Account Charges. If the currency used for your store orders is different from FedEx Currency, provide the conversion rate.   

  * **Conversion Rate** : Enter the conversion amount if you are using a currency different from the one that you set up in the WooCommerce base location. This amount gets multiplied by the shipping rates.  

  * **Adjustment:** Enter the conversion amount in case you have a different currency setup than the origin location currency. This amount will be multiplied by shipping rates. Leave it empty if no conversion is required.   

  * **Rates in Base Currency:** Select the checkbox for converting FedEx returned rates to the base currency of your store. For example, FedEx return rates are in USD. With this option, you can get the rates in the base currency EUR.

*Note: Conversion happens only if FedEx API provides the exchange rate.

**_Note:_**   
  
Conversion happens only if FedEx API provides the exchange rate.   
  

### Add FallBack Rate

Fallback: If FedEx returns no matching rates, offer this amount for shipping so that the user can still check out. Leave blank to disable.

### Set Minimum Order Value

  * Minimum order amount: This option allows you to set a minimum order amount which will trigger [Real-Time FedEx shipping rates](https://www.pluginhive.com/fedex-shipping-rates-on-your-woocommerce-store/). Any order below this amount will not have FedEx Shipping rates on the cart page.

### Add Minimum Shipping and Maximum Shipping Cost

Minimum Shipping Cost: If rates returned by FedEx is less than the Minimum Shipping Cost, then the customer will be charged Minimum Shipping Cost.

Maximum Shipping Cost: If rates returned by FedEx is greater than the Maximum Shipping Cost, then the customer will be charged Maximum Shipping Cost.

### Set Skip Products

**Skip products:** You can **Skip** all or selected products belonging to a particular shipping class while fetching rates and creating labels. This is achieved by the Skip Products function.

  * You can find **Skip Products** under the **General** tab.
  * First, assign a shipping class to the products you want to exclude. These products will then be skipped from shipping rate calculations based on their assigned shipping class.
  * The rates for skipped products won’t be included, and shipping labels will not be generated for them.

  * At least one product on the order is marked as a hazmat product

  * Create the shipment to generate the shipping label as normal flow.

  * Click **Ground Close Out (OP-950 & Manifest)** to generate the required close-out documents.

  * Click **OP_950** to open the label’s OP-950 document; it opens as an inline PDF in the browser.

## 16\. Enabling FedEx Special Services

### **FedEx Delivery Signature**

FedEx offers multiple options when a signature is required upon delivery. Depending on the type of shipment, the recipient must provide a valid signature to complete the delivery. If the required signature is not obtained, the shipment may be returned to the origin address (e.g., your warehouse).

To prevent such issues, FedEx provides three signature options:

  * Indirect Signature Required
  * Direct Signature Required
  * Adult Signature Required (required in the U.S. and Canada)

You can learn more about each option in the [FedEx Delivery Signature](https://www.pluginhive.com/fedex-pickup-and-special-delivery-options-for-your-woocommerce-shipments/).

Accessing FedEx Delivery Signature:

  * Select **FedEx® Delivery Signature Options** to choose the signature type from the dropdown list.

### **Configure FedEx Ground Economy (FedEx SmartPost)**

FedEx Ground® Economy (previously SmartPost) is an affordable shipping service designed for residential deliveries, especially useful for low-weight packages. It utilizes the U.S. Postal Service® for final delivery, helping you reach every U.S. address, including P.O. boxes, military APO, FPO, and DPO destinations.

To use FedEx Ground® Economy with your WooCommerce store, you must first enable the service and configure the required fields: **Hub ID** and **Indicia**.

#### Hub ID

This is required to route your shipment through the appropriate FedEx hub. The Hub ID represents the FedEx SmartPost hub responsible for initiating the shipment’s journey. You must select the correct hub based on your warehouse location.

Choose the closest hub to your fulfillment center from the dropdown.

#### Indicia

The **Indicia** represents the postal class or mail category of the shipment. It serves as a digital postage imprint and is essential for ensuring proper handling and accurate pricing of your packages. Select the appropriate indicia from the dropdown menu based on the content of your shipment.

For example, choose **“Media Mail”** for books or educational materials, or **“Presorted Bound Printed Matter”** for catalogs and magazines.

### **Dangerous Goods and Hazardous Materials**

The plugin supports Dangerous Goods and Hazardous Materials shipments. Configure this at the product level, under **Product Data > Shipping > FedEx Shipping Details**:

**Dangerous Goods Type:** select the option that matches your product. Available options:

  * **None:** the product is not a dangerous good — no DG data is sent to FedEx.
  * **Limited Quantities Commodities:** the package contains a dangerous good in a reduced, regulation-exempt quantity.
  * **Hazardous Materials:** a full HazMat shipment — shows the complete set of HazMat fields described below.
  * **Battery:** the product ships with an installed or separately packed lithium battery (see Battery Product Setup, below).
  * **ORM D:** Other Regulated Material — Domestic transport only.
  * **Small Quantity Exception:** the dangerous good is shipped in a small enough quantity to qualify for a regulatory exception.
  * **Excepted Quantities:** the dangerous good is packaged and labeled under the Excepted Quantities provision, allowing lighter documentation for very small quantities.
  * **Biological Substances Category B:** for biological substances classified as Category B (e.g., diagnostic specimens) under IATA/DOT rules.

* * *

Depending on the Dangerous Goods Type selected, the following fields appear:

  * **Regulation:** Shown for **Limited Quantities Commodities** and**Hazardous Materials**.
  * **Accessibility:** Shown for **Hazardous Materials** as well as**Limited Quantities Commodities**. 
  * **Cargo Aircraft Only:** Checkbox shown for **Limited Quantities Commodities, Hazardous Materials,** and**Battery** , marks the shipment as cargo-aircraft-only, not permitted on passenger aircraft.

* * *

The following additional fields appear specifically for **Hazardous Materials** and**Battery** :

  * **Subsidiary Classes:** additional hazard classes beyond the primary hazard class.
  * **Label Text:** the DOT diamond hazard label type, e.g. “FLAMMABLE LIQUID” — can also include a limited quantity or exemption number.
  * **Technical Name:** the technical name of the hazardous material, required for certain hazard classes.
  * **Packing Instructions:** the Dangerous Goods packing instructions, up to 40 characters, e.g. “DG”.
  * **Identification No.:** the DOT location ID number (UN or NA), e.g. “UN1088”.
  * **Packaging Group:** the hazardous material packaging group. Options: Default, I, II, III.
  * **Proper Shipping Name:** the hazardous material’s proper shipping name, e.g. “Acetal”. Up to three description lines of 50 characters each are allowed; these are formatted on the OP-950 form in 25-character columns (up to 6 printed lines).
  * **Hazard Class:** the DOT hazardous material class or division, e.g. “3”.

## **18\. Configure Integrations with FedEx Plugin**

Go to **WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping > FedEx > Integrations**.

The **Integrations** tab lets you connect the FedEx Shipping Plugin with supported WooCommerce plugins to automate shipment tracking, improve commercial invoices, and support multi-currency orders.

### **Share FedEx Tracking with PayPal**

If you use the **WooCommerce PayPal Payments** plugin, enable **Share FedEx Tracking with PayPal** to automatically send FedEx tracking information to PayPal whenever a shipment is created.

Once enabled:

  * FedEx tracking details are automatically shared with PayPal.
  * Eligible orders can benefit from **PayPal Seller Protection**.
  * Tracking information helps speed up payment hold releases where applicable.

* * *

**Note:** This option is available only when the **WooCommerce PayPal Payments** plugin is installed and activated.

* * *

### **WooCommerce Product Bundles Integration**

Bundle shipments are supported automatically. If you use the **WooCommerce Product Bundles** plugin, enable **Bundle: Include Child Products in Commercial Invoice** to list bundled child products individually on international commercial invoices.

When disabled, bundled products are grouped according to the Product Bundles plugin behaviour.

* * *

**Note:** This option requires the **WooCommerce Product Bundles** plugin to be installed and activated.

* * *

### **Multi-Currency Integration**

If your store uses a supported multi-currency plugin, enable **Use Order Currency for Shipments** to send the declared value and commercial invoice amounts in the customer’s order currency instead of your FedEx account currency.

The plugin automatically converts the values using your multi-currency plugin’s exchange rates.

* * *

**Note:** This setting can be overridden for individual orders from the WooCommerce Order Edit page.
