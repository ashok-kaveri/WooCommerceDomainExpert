# Display tax-inclusive WooCommerce UPS Rates at Checkout

In this small guide, we will tell if the **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin with Print Label](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** displays tax-inclusive WooCommerce UPS rates at the checkout page or not. Read more here.

**Customer** :

The WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin is not automatically showing rates inclusive of taxes at checkout. Can you help me?

* * *

**Support** :

Do you mean to say the shipping rates are not displayed inclusive of taxes?

For this, you need to go to **WooCommerce -- > Settings --> Taxes**. Then go to the relevant tax rate, and enable “Shipping” as in the below screenshot. This will add up tax in the shipping prices.
