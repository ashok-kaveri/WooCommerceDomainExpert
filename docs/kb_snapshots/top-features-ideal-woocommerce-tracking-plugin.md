## The Must-have Features of an Ideal WooCommerce Shipment Tracking Plugin

Every once in a while we get confused by the options available to us. Today we will discuss some of the most important features that will help you in choosing the best WooCommece shipment tracking plugin for your website.

### Support for Multiple Shipping Carrier Tracking

One of the key features to look for in a WooComerce Order Tracking plugin is the ability to support different shipping carriers. Today, most of the business owners are using some of the most popular shipping carriers in the world, such as**FedEx, UPS, DHL,** etc.,

An ideal WooCommerce tracking plugin must be flexible enough to allow business owners to add or remove a particular shipping carrier based on their convenience. There are a few carrier-based **[WooCommerce Shipping plugins](https://www.pluginhive.com/product-category/woocommerce-plugin/woocommerce-shipping/)** that are capable of providing shipment tracking.

But the only catch is that they work only with their respective shipping carrier. For instance, the **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** offers an extensive tracking feature that only works with UPS shipments.

### Share Tracking Details with Your Customers

It is very convenient for business owners to have the tracking details of each and every order they ship. However, it still doesn’t solve the problems for the customers.

Every time the customers want to track their orders, they will have to contact the owner of the store or customer support and ask for the tracking details of their order.

For a small to medium-scale business, spending this much time and money on entertaining such customer queries is never the best option. Instead, for an ideal WooCommerce tracking plugin, it must make the tracking details readily available to not only the store owner but to the customers too.

### Dedicated Shipment Tracking Lookup Area

An ideal WooCommerce shipment tracking plugin should allow you to add a personalised order tracking lookup page on your website. Your customers will be able to view the order tracking status on your Tracking Page instead of the shipping carrier’s website.

Customers should also be able to enter their tracking number whenever they want to check out the status of their orders. You can read this article on **[shipment tracking lookup page](https://www.pluginhive.com/live-shipment-tracking-for-your-ups-shipments-woocommerce-shipment-tracking/)** to know more about it.

### Customization of Tracking Message

Since we were discussing the customer and their convenience, one of the most important factors responsible for the success or failure of a business is how satisfied their customers are. Sending the tracking details to the customers is very convenient for them. However, adding a **personal touch** will only add to that convenience and will result in overall better customer experience.

An ideal WooCommerce tracking plugin must provide means to customize the way tracking details are sent to the customers. Be it an email template or a custom text message, it is always great to make your customers feel that you care about them.

### Automatically Mark WooCommerce Orders as Completed

**Automation** is one of the most important aspects that should be considered as the standard for an ideal WooCommerce tracking plugin. As we discussed earlier, with the number of customers and orders a WooCommerce store owner handles in a week, it becomes very tedious to handle every task manually.

Hence, even if there is a task as simple as completing the orders that can be automated with the use of a plugin, it could be a game-changer for most of business owners.

### Compatibility with Popular Shipping Software

Many store owners use the shipping software provided by the shipping companies to manage their shipments and provide tracking. If you are using a similar software like the UPS Worldship or Royal Mail Click & Drop, or any other third-party shipping software for e-commerce order fulfillment, you should be able to import the tracking data to WooCommerce.

A good WooCommerce shipment tracking plugin would allow you to create a CSV file containing the tracking data, upload it to your system, sync the details to WooCommerce orders and then mark the orders as **Completed.**

### Upload Shipment Tracking Data in Bulk

WooCommerce store owners can easily scale up their business to any part of the world. Moreover, there are businesses using WooCommerce that deal with more than 50 orders per week. Besides, having different shipping carriers providing different quality of service in various regions also forces the store owners to use not one, but multiple shipping carriers to fulfill their orders.

Hence, this requires advanced functionality where the business owner can handle the tracking data in bulk. One of the ways to do so is by using some mechanism to import the tracking details for hundreds of orders using a**CSV file.** This will help **decrease the time spent on just updating the tracking details** for the orders to a great extent.

### Automatically Schedule WooCommerce Tracking Import

As we discussed, how automation can help to decrease the workload of business owners, the ideal WooCommerce order tracking plugin must have a way to automate the task of updating the tracking details.

Not only that, the best option that a store owner can think of would be to**schedule the process of updating the tracking details** just once. This way it would be more convenient for the store owners to just schedule importing the tracking details once so that it can automatically start after some interval of time.

### Consolidated view of the WooCommerce Orders Tracking details

View all your orders in one place with live shipment tracking status, and make sure your shipment get delivered without any delay. This page will help you track and monitor all your shipments from a dedicated view within your WooCommerce store. 

The ideal WooCommerce shipment tracking plugin should also plugin provide a consolidated view of all the shipments based on their tracking status so that you can make sure your shipments are on time.

### Easy of Use

One of the most underrated features in any WooCommerce Shipping plugin is how easily it can be used by business owners. Dealing with the online business, the store owners may or may not be technically sound. Hence, the interface, as well as the working of the plugin, must have a pleasant and easier approach.

Being the ideal plugin a **[WooCommerce tracking plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-shipment-tracking-pro/)** must have an easy-to-use interface. Moreover, since WooCommerce covers a large geographical area, it would be great if the plugin supports **multiple languages**.

### Value For Money

As we discussed earlier, WooCommerce is used by small, medium as well as large-scale business owners. Hence, in the case of small to medium-scale business owners, which make up the most share of WooCommerce users, it is very important that the ideal WooCommerce tracking plugin must be budget-friendly**.**

Especially when it comes to small-scale business owners, who don’t really require the advanced features that most of the plugins provide. The cost of the ideal WooCommerce tracking plugin must directly reflect its **value for money**.
