## Solution using WooCommerce Shipping plugin for FedEx

Before we get into the actual solution, we would like to share a few more details regarding the B13A Document. This document is required to be completed and submitted for items that are valued at CAD $2,000 or more for countries other than the U.S., Puerto Rico, and U.S. Virgin Islands.

The **[WooCommerce Shipping plugin for FedEx](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-fedex-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** facilitates users to print the declaration number on the FedEx Shipping Label. This number can be further verified by government officials at a later step. Keep in mind that this number is generated by the Canadian government and is always unique for each shipment. You can read more about the important rules and regulations **[here](https://www.fedex.com/en-ca/shipping-services/international/regulatory/b13a.html)**.

The second important document you would need is the Declaration form. Since FedEx does not allow users/developers to fetch this document via FedEx APIs, you will have to get the documents manually. CBSA’s Canadian Automated Export Declaration (CAED) program is an electronic method that allows you to complete and transmit export information directly to the Government of Canada.

### Important notes following the FedEx B13A Document and related FedEx shipments

The CBSA strongly encourages exporters to complete and transmit the Export Declaration (B13A) using Statistics Canada and CBSA’s Canadian Automated Export Declaration (CAED) program. The CAED program is an electronic method that allows you to complete and transmit export information directly to the Government of Canada. The program is a fast, inexpensive, and easy way of reporting your exported goods.

  * A fast and easy way to report goods
  * Available at no extra charge
  * Provides a complete and accurate audit trail

Goods transiting the U.S. destined for the Rest of the World that meet the above criteria must also be reported on an export declaration. Be mindful of fluctuating exchange rates to ensure that the CAD $2,000 value is met.
