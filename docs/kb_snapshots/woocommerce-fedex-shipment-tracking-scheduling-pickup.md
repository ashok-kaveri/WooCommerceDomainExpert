In this article, we will show you how to use FedEx Tracking and FedEx Pickup features with the **[WooCommerce Shipping Plugin for FedEx with Print Label](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-fedex-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** and **[Shopify Ship, Rate, and Track for FedEx](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/shopify-fedex-shipping-app-with-print-label-tracking/)**. ## Can you provide FedEx tracking and raise FedEx Pickups in WooCommerce?

WooCommerce is a great platform to sell your products online. But to ship products to your customers, you’d have to display a Flat Rate or rates from a shipping carrier like **[FedEx](https://www.pluginhive.com/fedex-shipping/)** and **[UPS](https://www.pluginhive.com/ups-shipping/)**.

The **[WooCommerce FedEx Shipping Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-fedex-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** is the best option for WooCommerce users who want to ship items using FedEx services. With this plugin, you can display FedEx live shipping rates, print shipping labels, enable FedEx tracking, offer FedEx Freight services, schedule **[FedEx Pickups](https://www.pluginhive.com/fedex-pickup-and-special-delivery-options-for-your-woocommerce-shipments/)** , and more.

To have a better understanding of the features provided by this WooCommerce FedEx plugin, we should consider a real business case. For that, let us take the example of one of our customers trying to figure out the plugin.

* * *

#### **Solving a real business case**

**Customer’s request:** l would be happy to purchase this WooCommerce FedEx plugin if I could find the following features.

1) FedEx shipment tracking  
2) Creating shipments with FedEx C.O.D.  
3) Scheduling FedEx Pickup

We **[choose FedEx Priority Overnight or FedEx Standard Overnight](https://www.pluginhive.com/fedex-overnight-for-your-woocommerce/)** as per the weight slab, I am not able to find details if the product has a C.O.D. option.

We create a shipment that requires scheduling a product with a pickup location address, which gives us _a**pickup confirmation number**_. This pickup number is sent automatically to the local area manager, according to the area code and address. This helps them to pick up that product to deliver it.

Also, when we create a shipment from our website and schedule it from our panel, it will provide us AWB code to track product status and a pickup confirmation number to inform the area manager to pick up that product.

#### **Schedule FedEx Pickup in WooCommerce**

You can schedule FedEx pickups for your orders. This feature is seamlessly integrated with FedEx services and offers a great solution to your shipping ways. Let us look at the pickup setting section of the WooCommerce FedEx plugin. These options are available in the FedEx settings page under the WooCommerce settings.

* * *

* * *

Click on the **Enable Pickup** option to enable FedEx Pickup. You can specify a different address for the pick up by the option under the enable option.

Now under the Orders page, you would find all of your WooCommerce orders. You can either choose to enable FedEx Pickup individually or in bulk. Let’s take an example and raise a pickup request for the following order.

* * *

* * *

The process of enabling FedEx pickup for a particular order requires four steps –

  1. Select the order you want to raise the FedEx Pickup for
  2. Open the drop-down menu and select the **Request FedEx Pickup** option
  3. You then have to click on the **Apply** button
  4. Doing this refreshes the page and you can see the Requested comment under the FedEx Pickup column.

* * *

And there you go. You have successfully raised a FedEx Pickup request.

## Can you provide FedEx tracking and raise FedEx Pickups in Shopify?

Yes, you can, but only with the help of **[Shopify Ship, Rate,Track for FedEx](https://apps.shopify.com/fedex-shipping)**. The App comes with both FedEx shipment tracking and FedEx Pickup as built-in features and you can easily configure them.

#### **Using FedEx shipment tracking in Shopify**

Once you have configured the App, you should be able to fulfill an order. So for the sake of explanation, let’s assume we received an order where the customer has selected the FedEx Express Saver shipping method.

The App will create the shipment once you click on the Auto-Generate Label option. This will trigger the **[App to generate FedEx shipping labels](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/bulk-shipping-label-generation-made-easy-with-shopify-fedex-app/)** and enable order tracking for this order. In the image below, you can see the option to track the order and download the FedEx shipping label.

Upon clicking the Track Order option, the page takes you to the FedEx tracking page where you can track the order. Your customers too can track their order on this page which can be accessed directly from their order email.

* * *

You can use the **[Shopify Ship, Rate and Track for FedEx to raise FedEx pickups](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/shopify-fedex-shipment-tracking-and-scheduling-pickups/)** for your orders. The App allows you to set the Pickup start and company close time, choose the Pickup service and select the pickup location as well.
