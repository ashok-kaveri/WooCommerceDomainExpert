# La Poste Colissimo Shipping for WooCommerce

In this guide, we are going to explain to you the process of setting up La Poste Colissimo shipping on your WooCommerce store with **[WooCommerce Table Rate Shipping Pro](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-table-rate-shipping-pro-plugin/)**. Read along to know more.

## How to configure La Poste Colissimo shipping using WooCommerce Table Rate Shipping Pro?

The first thing you would need is a La Poste Colissimo account and a WooCommerce store. Once you have those ready, you can go ahead and download the **[WooCommerce Table Rate Shipping Pro plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-table-rate-shipping-pro-plugin/)**.

* * *

* * *

Once the installation is done, you would have to download the La Poste Colissimo CSV file and upload it to your **[WooCommerce Table Rate Shipping Pro plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-table-rate-shipping-pro-plugin/)** with the CSV upload.

#### [**Download La Poste Colissimo CSV**](https://www.pluginhive.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/la-poste-colissimo-data-file.csv.zip)

**Note:** The downloaded file would be a .zip file and you much extract and upload the .csv file.

As soon as the CSV file is uploaded, you should be able to see the service names under the Rule table. Please note that rates and services are pre-defined by Colissimo and we recommend you not make any changes. Here are some of the services supported and displayed by the **[WooCommerce Table Rate Shipping Pro](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-table-rate-shipping-pro-plugin/)**.

#### Expert France Colissimo & Expert Belgique Colissimo

* * *

#### Countries included in Zone1 Expert Outre-Mer Colissimo

#### Countries included in Zone2 Access Outre-Mer Colissimo

* * *

#### Countries included in Zone4 Expert International Colissimo

* * *

#### Countries included in Zone5 Expert International Colissimo

## Conclusion

 __To confirm if the rates are working, you must verify by placing an item in the Cart and checking the corresponding rate. The validation of the Price list is essential before actual shipping. You must also verify if the international shipping zones, Zone1, Zone2, Zone3, Zone4, Zone5, and Zone6 are correct.
