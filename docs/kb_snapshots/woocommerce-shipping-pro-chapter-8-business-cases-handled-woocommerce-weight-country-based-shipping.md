# WooCommerce Shipping Pro – Chapter 8 : Business cases handled by WooCommerce Weight and Country based shipping

In this article, we will detail out different business cases handled by WooCommerce Table Rate Shipping Pro Plugin. You can use this guide for your future reference.

### Shipping rules for the different business cases are :

  * Local shipping: fixed cost for the first .25/.5/.75/1kg.
  * Local shipping: after 1 kg, the cost for every 1 kg. weight rounded up to next 1 kg.
  * Neighboring shipping: fixed cost for the first .25/.5/.75/1kg.
  * Neighboring shipping: after 1 kg, the cost for every 1 kg. weight rounded up to next .5 kg.
  * Rest of the world: fixed cost $15 + cost for every kg. no weight rounding.
  * Free shipping: Any country after 10kg.
  * Default shipping: Any country after 100kg the cost for every kg. no weight rounding.

### Setup the shipping cost using WooCommerce Weight and Country based shipping.

With the following features setting up was a breeze.

  * Add Rule.
  * Remove Rule.
  * Duplicate Rule.
  * Last column “Note” will help to confirm the translated the business rule to text.
  * Display/Hide matrix columns.
