#### 2\. Choose the Parcel Packing method

This WooCommerce Fedex plugin is the perfect tool to optimize the box packaging process. You can configure the plugin in such a way that it will automatically determine how items should be packed in a certain box. If you wish to add your custom box, then you can easily do that as well.

In this plugin, you have the following three parcel-packing methods:

  * Calculate the shipping cost on the total weight
  * Pack items into boxes based on the weight and dimensions
  * Pack items individually

You can check out the following video to understand how the packaging methods can determine the shipping rates. In this video, we are showing you how the ‘weight-based’ and ‘pack items individually’ methods would affect the shipping rates differently.

#### 3\. Print the FedEx Shipping Label

This is one of the most important features of the WooCommerce FedEx Shipping plugin. This plugin will automatically print the shipping labels as soon as the customer completes an order on your WooCommerce store. The complete process is quite simple and can be done in one or two clicks. If you wish to make some changes in the shipment by adding another package, then you can choose to print the shipping labels for that particular order manually.

Based on your order details and specifications, the plugin sends the shipment details directly to your FedEx account and generates the shipping labels. You also have the liberty to choose the image type of the shipping plugin based on your printer configuration. As soon as the labels are generated, the postage will be automatically paid directly from the FedEx account.

#### 4\. FedEx Order Tracking

This plugin can achieve **[FedEx tracking](https://www.pluginhive.com/fedex-tracking-made-easy-for-woocommerce-users/)** for your shipments. It should be noted that the tracking ID is directly returned from FedEx and can be used to track the shipment on the official website as well.
