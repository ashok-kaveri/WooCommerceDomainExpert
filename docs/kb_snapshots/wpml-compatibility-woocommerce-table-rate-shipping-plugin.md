# WPML Compatibility with WooCommerce Table Rate Shipping

We have some good news! Our **[WooCommerce Table Rate Shipping Pro Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-table-rate-shipping-pro-plugin/)** is now compatible with the famous multilingual website, WPML. Over the last few days, our team has worked hard together with WPML’s team and we have come up with the latest integration.

* * *

## How does the WPML integration help PluginHive users?

With this integration, you can turn your store into a Multi-lingual one and ship to domestic and international locations without worrying about language translations.

Our team and WPML team worked together to conduct the compatibility testing of the plugin and made sure that the Table Rate Shipping Plugin works flawlessly with WPML.

* * *

### Why use WPML?

WPML is the most popular solution when it comes to converting your store into a multilingual one. Over 500,000 websites use it for creating a multilingual site. It is the industry standard that allows you to translate themes, plugins, theme options, posts, pages, widgets, custom post types, and everything else on WordPress.

In order to make your website multi-lingual so that even the shipping rates are shown in multiple languages, you would need to purchase **[WPML Plugin](https://wpml.org/purchase)**.

* * *

### Why use WooCommerce Table Rate Shipping Pro?

**The[WooCommerce Table Rate Shipping Pro plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-table-rate-shipping-pro-plugin/)** by PluginHive helps you to define custom shipping rates for your online store. Once you configure the plugin, the shipping rates can be shown in the cart/checkout page.

You can check out the WPML compatibility page for our plugin. Here and also read the documentation: **[WPML Compatibility for WooCommerce Table Rate Shipping Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/using-wpml-woocommerce-table-rate-shipping-pro-plugin-multilingual-sites/)**.

If you need any help setting up table rate/conditional shipping on your WooCommerce store. Feel free to **[Contact PluginHive Customer Support](https://www.pluginhive.com/support/)**. We would be more than happy to help.
