You may sell products with similar physical attributes or items of varying shapes. Regardless, when it comes to optimizing your shipments, it’s always best to have the right boxes prepared in advance.

If you have a product that doesn’t fit into the standard FedEx boxes, you can create your own custom box. Let’s explore more about the different types of packaging FedEx offers. 

The FedEx WooCommerce Shipping plugin provides three parcel packing methods:

  * Pack into boxes with predefined weights and dimensions
  * Pack items individually
  * Weight-based packing, which calculates shipping costs based on the total weight

* * *

### Pack into boxes with weights and dimensions

In the image below, you can see that the **“Pack into boxes with weights and dimensions”** method has been selected. This method packs multiple items into predefined boxes while respecting both the weight and dimension limits.

Additionally, you can choose a **Packing Algorithm** to control how items are arranged:

  * **Default: Volume-Based Packing** – Packs items based on available volume.
  * **Stack First Packing** – Tries to stack items vertically for better space usage.
  * **Based on Volume Used × Item Count** – Optimizes packing by balancing item count and volume used.

This packaging approach ensures efficient use of box space while keeping shipments within limits. For instance, if you’re shipping different products with different dimensions and weights, the plugin calculates the optimal way to fit them into one or more boxes. If everything can’t fit in a single box, it automatically splits the items into multiple packages.

* * *

### Pack items individually

This is the simplest way of packing items or products into boxes. Let us assume that you have five products, and each one of them has different weights and dimensions.

If you choose to pack items individually, then the plugin will consider each item separately and will pack them into separate boxes. However, the plugin would choose each box based on the weight and dimensions of individual products.

The plugin will add all the shipping costs of each box and will show the total [FedEx shipping rate](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/understanding-fedex-shipping-rates-in-your-shopify-store/) on the Cart/Checkout page. For example, if you have a product that costs $22 and the customer orders two quantities, then the total cost would show up as $44 ($22+$22).

* * *

### Weight-based packing method

In weight-based packing, items are grouped into packages based solely on their weight. As shown in the image below, you can set a **maximum package weight** , for example, 10 lbs. Once this limit is exceeded, the plugin automatically starts a new package.

You can also choose how items are packed using one of three options:

  * Pack heavier items first
  * Pack lighter items first
  * Pack purely divided by weight

Choose carefully, as this setting can impact shipping rates.

[Weight-based packing](https://www.pluginhive.com/knowledge-base/weight-based-packing-woocommerce-fedex-shipping-plugin/)**** is recommended, as it often results in lower shipping costs, especially when multiple items are in the cart. This is demonstrated in this video comparing weight-based and individual packing methods. While rates are similar for a single item, weight-based packing becomes more cost-effective as quantity increases.

This method also ignores item dimensions, making it ideal for lightweight, bulky products, like cotton-stuffed toys, where weight is the main cost factor.
