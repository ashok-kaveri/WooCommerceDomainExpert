# Why UPS Shipping Methods are not listed under WooCommerce Shipping Zones?

With this guide, we’ll tell you why WooCommerce Shipping Methods for UPS services are not listed under the WooCommerce Shipping Zones. Read along to know more.

## When do you need to configure the Shipping Zones?

The**[WooCommerce Shipping Zones](https://www.pluginhive.com/woocommerce-shipping-zones-ultimate-guide/)** is a great feature offered by WooCommerce as it lets you create geographical regions that can be used to categorize actual shipping areas.

Using the WooCommerce Shipping Zones, you can calculate shipping rates for shipping methods like Flat rate, Free shipping, and Local pickup. So you are limited by these shipping methods and thus, have only a little to play around with.

Advanced WooCommerce shipping plugins offer more shipping options and let you all types of shipping scenarios like conditional shipping or table rate shipping.

* * *

## Why UPS Shipping Services are not shown under WooCommerce Shipping Zones?

The **[WooCommerce UPS Shipping Plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/)** is a brilliant solution for store owners wanting to ship items via UPS services. The plugin offers real-time shipping rates directly from the UPS APIs based on the serviceable areas defined by UPS itself. So the UPS shipping services aren’t available under shipping zones, specifically.

WooCommerce shipping zones become redundant in this case and tend to be a complete overkill, so to speak. Since the shipping rates are calculated by UPS, there’s no need to add another layer of **[WooCommerce shipping zones](https://www.pluginhive.com/woocommerce-shipping-zones-ultimate-guide/)** to the process. Doing otherwise will only result in configuring the same shipping settings multiple times for each of your shipping zones.

In addition, the [**WooCommerce UPS Shipping plugin**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-ups-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/) is an independent shipping plugin and offers all the necessary features requires to set up an order delivery system. Apart from the live shipping rates on the cart/checkout, you get label generation, live shipment tracking, freight shipping, packaging options, etc., things you don’t get in WooCommerce alone.
