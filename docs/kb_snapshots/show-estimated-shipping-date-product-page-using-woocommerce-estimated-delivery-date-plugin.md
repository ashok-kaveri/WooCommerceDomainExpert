# Show WooCommerce Estimated Delivery Date on the Product Page

One of the smartest ways to give a boost to your online sales is by providing delivery estimation to the customers. It is added features like this that make a huge difference when it comes to close competition. In WooCommerce there is no method to provide delivery estimation, by default. According to a WooCommerce store owner,”…Hello, I want to confirm something about the premium version of your plugin. Is there a way to show the estimated shipping date on the product page?_**I do not have any shipping classes and I have 4000 products. How can we do this..? Please help… Thank you..!**_ “

In this article, we will be covering [**WooCommerce Estimated Delivery Date plugin**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/estimated-delivery-date-plugin-woocommerce/) and how using this plugin WooCommerce store owners can provide delivery estimation not only on the cart and checkout page but also on the product page.

* * *

## Create Shipping Classes

WooCommerce Estimated Delivery Date plugin allows store owners to provide delivery estimates to their customers. Using this plugin customers will be able to see the delivery estimates of the products on the car page, checkout page, and even the product page. However, in order to work properly, the plugin requires**shipping classes** to be set up. Hence, if you want to show the shipping estimates so you can easily create a shipping class and assign the product to that shipping class. The image below shows the shipping classes along with the delivery days assigned to them in the plugin settings.

Once you are done with the shipping classes and assigning delivery days to them, the shipping estimate will be visible to your customers not only on the **cart and checkout page but also on the product page**. This way customers can get the delivery estimate well before even adding the product to the cart. The image below shows the delivery estimate on the product page itself.
