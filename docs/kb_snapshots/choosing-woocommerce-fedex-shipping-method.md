## Understanding FedEx Shipping Method

Currently, **[FedEx](https://www.pluginhive.com/fedex-shipping/)** offers a comprehensive range of services to more than 220 countries and territories. These services vary on the basis of the location and region. For example, services like the **[FedEx Standard Overnight](https://www.pluginhive.com/fedex-standard-overnight-for-woocommerce/)** works in domestic countries but does not work in the United Kingdom regions. Let us try to understand this in a better way.

Below is an image that shows the different FedEx services in some parts of the world. If you take a closer look, you would notice that there are only three services available in Madagascar while there are five services in South Africa. This means that you can choose a certain service that is valid for some other region but not valid for your region.

* * *

* * *

Another important thing to note is that some countries like Bolivia do not have postal codes. This is one of the reasons why some FedEx Services might not work in particular countries. Similarly, there are other important parameters that determine the shipping services in a particular region.

The kind of service will also depend upon the physical dimension of the package. There are certain rules that define whether a package would be selected as Freight or would be delivered as a normal order. For instance, if your shipment qualifies as LTL (less-than-truckload), then you would need to select the Freight options available for that particular service.

### Configure FedEx Shipping Methods based on your need

FedEx Ground is one of the most famous and efficient shipping services offered by FedEx. They use FedEx Ground for commercial (business address) deliveries and FedEx Ground Home Delivery for Deliveries to residential addresses. Please note that the FedEx API picks up the value GROUND_HOME_DELIVERY for the residential Ground deliveries and thus the plugin works accordingly. Regardless of the service name, this plugin allows you to change or define your own custom shipping names to avoid confusion.

In the image given above, you can see that we have defined the GROUND_HOME_DELIVERY with FedEx Home Delivery. However, this feature does not affect the shipping rates on the Cart/Checkout page.

### Hiding shipping method on the Cart/Checkout page

If you combine the WooCommerce FedEx Shipping plugin with the [**Hide Shipping Methods Based on Shipping Class and Zone**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/hide-woocommerce-shipping-methods-and-rate-adjustment/), then you would be able to show only the shipping services that you would want to offer. This is a small but nifty plugin that might help you implement a particular business scenario. For example, if the FedEx Priority Overnight rate is higher in Georgia, but is comparatively cheaper in the New York region then you can define the shipping zones in the **Hide Shipping Methods plugin** accordingly. This way when a customer from Georgia selects a particular product, he or she would not be able to see that service. However, a customer from New York would be able to choose this service and buy the product.

The plugin also allows you to change the shipping service for an order that has been placed. So, even if the order chosen by the customer is not favorable for you, you can change it at a later stage.

For more information, please read: [**How to Hide WooCommerce Shipping Methods?**](https://www.pluginhive.com/hide-woocommerce-shipping-methods-complete-tutorial/)
