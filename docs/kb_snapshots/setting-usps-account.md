# Setting up USPS Account

In this tutorial, we will show you how you can apply for a USPS shipping Account to use it with PluginHive **[WooCommerce shipping](https://www.pluginhive.com/product-category/woocommerce-plugin/woocommerce-shipping/)** solutions and get real-time shipping rates from USPS on your WooCommerce store.

## Register for USPS Web Tools

  1. Go to <https://www.usps.com/business/web-tools-apis/welcome.htm> (Not the URL used for logging into My USPS account)
  2. Under **Sign Up** , click **Register Now**.
  3. Enter all the required details in the given registration form and click on **Submit**.

After registration, you will receive an email containing your unique **Web Tools User ID and Password**. It also includes instructions for the next steps and requests for additional permissions.
