# Will the WooCommerce Estimated Delivery Date plugin work with Shipping Classes and Shipping Zones, both at a time?

Showing estimated delivery dates to your customers is a very good approach to increasing the credibility of your WooCommerce store. The **[Estimated Delivery Date plugin](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/estimated-delivery-date-plugin-woocommerce/) **is an amazing plugin that lets you do that. Furthermore, it facilitates the choice to offer different estimated delivery dates for different shipping zones as well as shipping classes. This feature can prove to be quite useful for you in many business scenarios.

However, some of our customers, upon purchasing this plugin, cannot figure out how to do that. According to Hugo,”… _**Is it possible to automatically present the possible delivery date, depending on the zone and the shipping class I have selected..? Imagine the following scenario – I have an order for Zone 2 (Europe) and the estimated delivery time for this zone is 5 days. I placed the order today 02-11-2017, this plugin automatically presents the calculation of the estimated date of delivery that in this example would be 07-11-2017. Does this plugin calculate it by itself..?”**_

Let’s see whether the plugin supports shipping classes and shipping zones.

## Shipping Zones

The plugin supports both the shipping classes and shipping zones. The store owners can easily select the shipping zone or the shipping classes from the settings. The image below shows the plugin settings showing both shipping classes and shipping zone.

Once you click on the shipping zone option, you will get the following settings page.

Here you can easily add delivery days to the shipping zones based on your requirements.

## Shipping Classes

As for the shipping class, you need to click on the shipping class option in the plugin settings. The image below shows the shipping classes page of the plugin settings. Here you can easily add the number of delivery days based on your requirements for every shipping class.

## Summary…

This article covers the WooCommerce Estimated Delivery Date Shipping plugin. Using this article, WooCommerce store owners will be able to define an estimated delivery date by taking into consideration any holidays in the week. These holidays will be considered for either the vendor or the customer or for both vendors and customers, based on the settings.
