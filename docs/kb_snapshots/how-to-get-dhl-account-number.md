# How to get DHL Account Number?

In this guide, we will show you how to get DHL Account Number that can be used to automate DHL shipping with PluginHive’s [**WooCommerce DHL Shipping plugin**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/woocommerce-dhl-shipping-plugin-with-print-label/) and [**Shopify DHL Shipping app**](https://www.pluginhive.com/product/shopify-dhl-shipping-app-rates-shipping-labels-tracking/).

## Create a DHL Account

  
**Step 1** : Visit****[**DHL website**](https://mydhl.express.dhl/us/en/home.html) and choose which account you want to open with DHL. You can open either a Personal account or a Business account.

* * *

* * *

  
**Step 2** : Enter your personal/business details and click on submit, as shown below.

> After entering the details above, DHL will review the details and create an account for you.

## Create an XML PI account to get the DHL test credentials

  
**Step 3** : In order to have a test DHL account number, visit the **[DHL Express XML Developer Portal](https://xmlportal.dhl.com/register)** page and get registered, as shown below.

* * *

* * *

  
Step 4: Enter the details shown below along with the DHL Express account details that you created in the first step.

* * *

* * *

  
**Step 5** : After saving these details you will receive the DHL Test Account credentials via email, as shown below.

* * *

* * *

  
You can use the Test credentials to test the shipping solution for getting live shipping rates, generating test/sample DHL shipping labels, and sample tracking numbers.

> Once the testing is completed, you will require DHL Production Credentials to ship your orders with DHL.

* * *

### Get DHL Production Access for the UK and Rest of the World

After creating the test credentials, you need to contact the DHL account manager. The account manager must request integration via DHL Pre-sales Department. The pre-sales department will then provide you with the DHL production credentials.

* * *

### Get DHL Production Access for the United States of America

In order to get the DHL production credentials, you need to write to [**[email protected]**](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#176f7a7b657266627264636457737f7b3974787a), along with your full account details like account number, region, address, etc. to get API Access.
